## Overview

AI transforms sales outreach from a manual, high-volume activity into a precision targeting system. This workflow covers the full pipeline: finding prospects → researching them → writing personalized emails → following up → booking meetings — with AI at every step.

## Workflow Architecture

```
[Prospecting] → [Research] → [Personalized Outreach] → [Follow-up] → [Meeting Booking] → [CRM Update]
```

## Step 1: AI-Powered Prospecting

| Tool | Function | Cost |
|------|----------|------|
| **Apollo.io** | AI prospect database with intent signals | Free / $49/m |
| **Clay** | AI enrichment and data aggregation | $149/m |
| **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** | Lead discovery | $99/m |
| **Perplexity Pro** | Company and industry research | $20/m |

**AI prompt for initial research:**
```
Research [Company Name] and provide:
1. Their main product/service and target customer
2. Recent news (funding, product launches, hiring)
3. Key decision-makers and their backgrounds
4. Potential pain points relevant to [your product]
5. Cultural fit indicators (remote, startup, enterprise)
```

## Step 2: Personalized Email Generation

Use Claude or ChatGPT for hyper-personalized outreach:

```
Write a cold email to [Name], [Title] at [Company].

Context from research:
- [Company] just raised $10M Series A
- [Name] previously led growth at [Previous Company]
- [Company] uses [Tech Stack]

Our product [Your Product] helps [value prop].

Requirements:
- Reference their recent funding round
- Mention their specific challenge (based on research)
- Keep under 150 words
- Ask a specific, low-friction question
- No generic flattery
```

## Step 3: Multi-Channel Follow-up Sequence

| Touchpoint | Day | Channel | Message Type |
|------------|:---:|---------|-------------|
| 1 | 0 | Email | Initial personalized outreach |
| 2 | 3 | LinkedIn | Connection request (mention email) |
| 3 | 7 | Email | Case study relevant to their industry |
| 4 | 10 | LinkedIn | Comment on their post |
| 5 | 14 | Email | Breakup email (final value proposition) |

## Step 4: Automated Meeting Booking

1. AI qualifies inbound interest (reply analysis)
2. Auto-sends Calendly or Cal.com link
3. Syncs meeting to CRM
4. Sends pre-meeting briefing to sales rep (AI-generated prospect summary)

## FAQ

**Does AI personalization really work better than templates?** Yes — personalized emails with specific company references get 40% higher response rates than template-based emails.

**How many touchpoints before giving up?** Research suggests 5-7 touchpoints across multiple channels. After that, move to long-term nurture.

**What's the best AI tool for sales outreach?** Claude generates the most natural-sounding outreach emails. ChatGPT is faster for volume. Combine Claude for quality with Mailmeteor for delivery.

**Should I disclose AI in sales emails?** Not necessary for the writing, but be transparent about automation in the process. Prospects appreciate knowing you used AI to research them specifically.
