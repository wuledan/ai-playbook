## Overview

Not all AI image generators are created equal. Midjourney excels at artistic imagery, DALL-E at photorealism, and Stable Diffusion at customization — but what about **brand assets** and **typography**? For those, you need specialized tools.

This tutorial covers two AI art platforms that solve specific design problems:

- **Recraft** — The best AI tool for vector graphics, brand kits, and scalable designs. Unlike pixel-based generators, Recraft produces editable SVG vectors that you can use in logos, icons, and illustrations. It's the go-to tool for designers who need production-ready brand assets.

- **Ideogram** — The clear leader in AI-generated typography. While most AI image tools struggle with text rendering (garbled letters, misspelled words), Ideogram gets it right. It's perfect for posters, social media graphics, presentations, and any design where text matters.

**Who this is for:** Content creators, marketers, small business owners, and designers who want to create professional visuals without Adobe subscriptions.

## Prerequisites

- Free accounts at [recraft.ai](https://recraft.ai) and [ideogram.ai](https://ideogram.ai)
- Basic understanding of design concepts (color, composition — not required but helpful)
- No prior experience with AI art tools needed

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Part 1: Creating Vector Brand Assets with Recraft

Recraft stands out because it generates **vectors**, not pixels. This means your designs scale to any size without losing quality — perfect for logos, icons, and print materials.

#### Step 1: Set Up Your Recraft Workspace

1. Go to [recraft.ai](https://recraft.ai) and sign up (Google login supported)
2. Start on the free plan (20 credits/day, enough for basic needs)
3. The interface has three main areas:
   - **Left panel** — Project files and templates
   - **Center** — Canvas/preview area
   - **Right panel** — Generation controls (style, format, parameters)

#### Step 2: Generate a Vector Logo

1. Select **"Generate"** from the top menu
2. Choose **"Vector art"** as the output type — this is Recraft's specialty
3. In the style dropdown, pick from Recraft's curated styles:
   - **"Flat illustration"** — Clean, modern, perfect for tech brands
   - **"Line art"** — Minimalist, great for icons
   - **"3D vector"** — Depth and dimension for premium brands
   - **"Retro"** — Vintage aesthetic for nostalgic branding
4. For a first try, write a prompt like:

```
Modern coffee shop logo with a stylized coffee cup and steam forming a leaf shape. Minimalist line art style, warm earth tones (#8B4513, #D2B48C, #F5F5DC). Clean shapes, suitable for both digital and print.
```

5. Click **Generate** — Recraft produces 4 variations
6. Hover over any result and click **"Edit"** to refine:
   - Change colors using the color palette picker
   - Adjust composition (scale, position, rotation)
   - Modify individual elements (Recraft's vector edits are non-destructive)

**Pro tip:** For the best vector results, include material keywords in your prompt: "flat vector," "solid colors," "clean paths," "minimalist silhouette."

#### Step 3: Create a Cohesive Brand Kit

Recraft's **Brand Kit** feature is what makes it superior for branding:

1. Click **Brand Kit** in the left sidebar
2. Add your brand colors:
   - Click **Add Color** and enter hex codes (e.g., #2C3E50, #E74C3C, #ECF0F1)
   - Name your palette (e.g., "Primary," "Secondary," "Accent")
3. Upload or generate reference assets:
   - Your existing logo (if you have one)
   - Mood board images for style reference
4. Set your brand style:
   - Font preferences (Recraft integrates with Google Fonts)
   - Illustration style (choose from the style library)
5. **Save Brand Kit**

Now, any new generation can use your brand kit as a reference:

```
[Layout with title "New Product Launch" in modern serif, 
icon composition showing a lightbulb and gear in flat vector style, 
use brand kit colors. Minimal. Professional.]
```

Recraft will apply your brand colors and style consistently across all generated assets.

#### Step 4: Export Vectors for Production

Recraft offers multiple export formats. Choose based on your use case:

| Use Case | Format | Settings |
|----------|--------|----------|
| Web/UI | SVG | Default (editable in Figma/Illustrator) |
| Print | EPS or PDF | High resolution, CMYK color |
| Social media | PNG | 1080×1080px, transparent background |
| Animation | Lottie JSON | For motion graphics |

**To export:**
1. Click the download icon on your design
2. Select format
3. For SVG: check "Maintain editability" — this keeps layers and groups intact
4. The SVG can be opened in Figma, Illustrator, or Inkscape for further editing

**Real-world test:** Export a logo as SVG, import it into Figma, and try editing individual elements. You'll find each shape is a separate editable path — not a flattened image. This is the power of Recraft's vector generation.

#### Step 5: Advanced Recraft Techniques

**Style consistency across generations:**
Use Recraft's **"Reference image"** feature. Upload a previous design you like, and Recraft generates new variations that match its style. This is invaluable for creating a cohesive brand icon set.

**Mockups:**
Recraft can place your designs into realistic mockups:
1. Select your finished design
2. Click **"Create mockup"**
3. Choose from: T-shirt, business card, storefront sign, mobile screen
4. Recraft generates a photorealistic mockup in seconds

**Recolor tool:**
For rapid A/B testing of color schemes:
1. Open a finished design
2. Click **Recolor** in the editing toolbar
3. Try "Complementary," "Monochromatic," or "Triadic" schemes
4. Save multiple versions for client presentations

### Part 2: Creating Typography Posters with Ideogram

Ideogram has become the go-to tool for AI typography after multiple generations of improvement. Its text rendering accuracy is unmatched.

#### Step 6: Understand Ideogram's Strengths

Ideogram excels at:
- **Rendering text inside images** — Banners, posters, social media quotes
- **Logos with text** — Company names integrated with icons
- **Posters and flyers** — Headlines, subtext, and body copy
- **Informational graphics** — Data visualization with labels

The trick: Ideogram handles English text well, and supports Spanish, French, German, and Japanese. Avoid complex formatting (superscript, subscripts, mixed text directions).

#### Step 7: Generate Your First Typography Poster

1. Go to [ideogram.ai](https://ideogram.ai) and sign up
2. Navigate to the **Create** tab
3. Set your aspect ratio:
   - `1:1` — Instagram square
   - `4:3` — Standard poster
   - `9:16` — Mobile/Stories
   - `16:9` — YouTube thumbnails, desktop wallpapers
4. Choose a magic prompt or write your own:

```
Poster design for "Summer Music Festival 2026"
Headline text in bold modern sans-serif font: "SUMMER BEATS"
Subtitle: "June 15-17 | Central Park"
Three musical notes floating upward in vibrant gradient colors
Electric yellow to hot pink background with subtle sound wave patterns
Style: event poster, Adobe Illustrator quality, clean professional layout
```

5. **Critical:** Ideogram has a special syntax for text emphasis — use uppercase for text you want to render exactly:

```
Poster with text "SALE" in big bold red letters, text "50% OFF" below in yellow, 
background dark blue with confetti. 
```

6. Click **Generate** — wait 10-20 seconds

Ideogram produces 4 variations. Examine each for text accuracy. If letters are garbled, regenerate with:
- More specific font instructions ("Helvetica, bold, sans-serif")
- Shorter text strings
- Clear separation between text and background

#### Step 8: Refine Typography Results

Ideogram's text rendering isn't perfect every time. Use these techniques to improve results:

**Technique 1: Magic Prompt mode**
Toggle **Magic Prompt** on. Ideogram's AI enhances your prompt, adding style and composition instructions. This often improves text rendering dramatically.

**Technique 2: Use "text" as a keyword**
Explicitly include "text" in your prompt:

```
Album cover featuring text "MIDNIGHT" in large silver metallic letters,
smaller text "Track 1" below, dark blue starry background, neon glow
```

**Technique 3: Specify font characteristics**
Ideogram respects font descriptions:

```
Quote poster: text "CREATE WITHOUT LIMITS" in serif font, elegant gold letters,
text "— 2026 Manifesto" below in smaller italic sans-serif
```

**Technique 4: Negative prompts**
Ideogram supports negative prompting to avoid common issues:

```
[-] blurry text, misspelled words, small unreadable text, distorted letters
```

#### Step 9: Batch Create Social Media Templates

One of Ideogram's most practical uses is batch-creating branded templates:

1. Create a base design with your brand colors and fonts
2. Generate once — get a template you like
3. Use **"Remix"** (click the remix icon on any result)
4. Change just the text and regenerate:

- Variation 1: "MONDAY MOTIVATION" with morning sky background
- Variation 2: "TECH TIP TUESDAY" with circuit pattern
- Variation 3: "WISDOM WEDNESDAY" with faded book background

Each remix preserves the overall layout while updating the text and matching imagery.

#### Step 10: Export and Use Your Designs

Ideogram exports all common formats:

| Format | Best For | Max Resolution (Free) |
|--------|----------|----------------------|
| PNG | Web, social media | 1024×1024 |
| JPG | Web, email | 1024×1024 |
| PNG (HD) | Print | 4096×4096 (Pro) |

**For web use:** PNG with transparent backgrounds. Note that Ideogram doesn't directly support transparent backgrounds — but you can use a third-party background remover (remove.bg, Photoshop) to isolate the text/design.

**For print:** Download the highest resolution available. Ideogram Pro subscribers get 4K exports suitable for poster printing.

## Troubleshooting

### Recraft vectors have unwanted details

Reduce your prompt specificity. Instead of "detailed illustration," try "minimalist vector with solid colors." Use the style presets — Recraft's "Line art" and "Flat illustration" presets produce the cleanest vectors.

### Ideogram misspells words

- Keep text short (1-5 words works best)
- Use ALL CAPS for text you need rendered exactly
- Try Magic Prompt mode
- Add "exact spelling" to your negative prompt
- Regenerate 2-3 times with the same prompt — sometimes the first generation has errors but subsequent ones don't

### Designs look generic

Add specific style references: "in the style of Art Deco," "vaporwave aesthetic," "Swiss design style 1960s." Recraft and Ideogram both recognize art movements and style references.

## FAQ

### Is Recraft better than Adobe Illustrator for vectors?

Recraft is better for **generating** new vector designs from scratch. Illustrator is better for **editing** those designs with fine control. Best workflow: Recraft to generate → export SVG → Illustrator/Figma for polish.

### Can Ideogram handle Chinese characters?

Ideogram's text rendering works best with Latin scripts. For Chinese, Japanese, or Korean characters, results vary. Test with short text (2-4 characters) and specific font instructions.

### Are Recraft vectors truly editable in other apps?

Yes. SVG exports from Recraft maintain individual shapes, layers, and paths. When opened in Figma or Illustrator, you can recolor, resize, and edit individual elements. This is unique among AI image generators.

Recraft: Free tier (20 credits/day), Pro $15/month (500 credits). Ideogram: Free tier (25 credits/day), Pro $20/month (unlimited). Combined, $35/month for professional design capabilities — less than a single Adobe CC app.
