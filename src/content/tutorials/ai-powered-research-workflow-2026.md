## Introduction

Research is one of the areas where AI delivers the most dramatic productivity gains. A task that once took days — literature review, cross-referencing, synthesis — can now be done in hours or even minutes with the right workflow.

This guide walks you through an end-to-end AI research workflow that works for academic papers, market research, competitive analysis, and content creation.

## Step 1: Define Your Research Question

Before touching any AI tool, write down your research question clearly. Good questions lead to good results.

**Bad:** "Tell me about AI in healthcare"
**Good:** "What are the three most significant clinical applications of large language models in radiology as of 2026?"

## Step 2: Deep-Dive with Perplexity

Perplexity Pro is ideal for the initial exploration phase because it searches the web in real-time and provides citations.

**How to use it:**
1. Enter your research question as a query
2. Follow up with "What are the key papers/studies on this?"
3. Use "What are the main debates or disagreements in this field?"
4. Ask "Who are the leading researchers or organizations working on this?"

Save the conversation as a collection for later reference.

## Step 3: Build a Knowledge Base with NotebookLM

NotebookLM excels at working with specific source documents. After Perplexity gives you an overview, collect actual sources and feed them into NotebookLM.

**Workflow:**
1. Download relevant papers, articles, and reports
2. Upload them to a new NotebookLM notebook
3. Ask NotebookLM to identify key themes, contradictions, and gaps
4. Use the "Audio Overview" feature for a podcast-style summary of your sources

**Pro tip:** Create one notebook per research topic. Upload 10-20 sources per notebook for the best synthesis.

## Step 4: Synthesis with ChatGPT

Use ChatGPT for the final synthesis phase. Give it the output from Perplexity and NotebookLM, then ask it to:

- "Create a structured outline for a 3000-word article on this topic"
- "Identify the three strongest arguments for each position"
- "Generate a summary suitable for an executive audience"

**Key technique:** Use Custom Instructions to set your context. Tell ChatGPT your audience, tone, and depth requirements upfront.

## Step 5: Verification

AI can hallucinate. Always verify:
- **Direct quotes** — Search quoted text to confirm they're real
- **Statistics** — Find the original source of any specific number
- **Attributions** — Ensure claims attributed to specific people/organizations are accurate

Use Perplexity to verify each claim by pasting it as a query.

## Step 6: Output & Citation

Export your final output with a proper citation format. NotebookLM's auto-citation feature is excellent for this. ChatGPT's "Write a properly cited version" prompt also works well.

## Complete Workflow Summary

```
Question → Perplexity (explore) → Sources → NotebookLM (analyze) 
→ Synthesis → ChatGPT (write) → Verification → Perplexity (check) → Output
```

## Final Tips

1. **Start broad, then narrow** — Begin with Perplexity, get the landscape, then dive deep with NotebookLM
2. **Keep source files organized** — NotebookLM notebooks become your research archive
3. **Always verify** — The #1 rule of AI-assisted research
4. **Iterate** — Your first synthesis is rarely your last. Use follow-up prompts to refine
5. **Document your process** — Save your prompts and results for future reference
