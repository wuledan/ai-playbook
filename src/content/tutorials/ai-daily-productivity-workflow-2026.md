## The Daily Productivity Problem

Knowledge workers spend an average of 30% of their workday on repetitive tasks: triaging email, scheduling meetings, taking notes, and tracking action items. These tasks don't require your unique skills — and AI can handle them.

This guide builds an integrated productivity system using 4 AI tools.

## Step 1: AI Email Triage

Start your day with an AI email assistant.

**Setup:**
1. Connect your email to Shortwave or enable Superhuman AI
2. Configure your triage categories: "Urgent Client," "Internal," "Newsletters," "Spam"
3. Set up auto-responses for common queries: "Can we meet?", "What's the status?", "Can you review this?"

**Morning routine:**
- Open email → AI has already sorted into priority stacks
- Review "Urgent" first (usually 3-5 emails after AI filtering)
- Scan "Internal" for quick responses
- Delete "Newsletters" stack in one click
- Time spent: 5-7 minutes vs 20-30 minutes before

## Step 2: Calendar Optimization with AI

Let AI optimize your schedule.

**Setup with Motion:**
1. Connect your calendar and task list
2. Define "focus time" blocks (2+ hours, no meetings)
3. Set meeting preferences (duration, advance notice, buffer time)
4. Motion auto-schedules tasks into available slots

**Pro tip:** Block 9-11am every day as "deep work" — Motion will never schedule meetings there.

## Step 3: Automated Meeting Notes

Never take manual meeting notes again.

**Setup with Otter.ai:**
1. Connect Otter to your calendar — it auto-joins meetings
2. Upload the names and roles of regular attendees
3. Enable "Action Item Detection"
4. Set up automatic sharing to Notion or Slack

**After each meeting:**
- Otter sends a summary with action items
- Review for accuracy (takes 30 seconds)
- Action items are already in your task system

## Step 4: AI Task Management

**Notion AI Workflow:**
1. Create a "Daily Inbox" database page
2. Use the "AI Auto-triage" template (summarizes and categorizes incoming items)
3. Configure a "Daily Review" page that AI populates with:
   - Yesterday's completion status
   - Today's priority tasks (auto-sourced from email/meetings/calendar)
   - Suggested focus areas based on deadlines

**Evening review (5 min):**
- Ask Notion AI: "What were my top 3 accomplishments today?"
- Review tomorrow's auto-generated priority list
- Adjust if needed

## Step 5: Weekly AI Retrospective

End each week with an AI-powered review.

**Prompt for ChatGPT/Claude:**
```
Based on this week's data — meeting notes, completed tasks, and email volume:
1. What patterns do you see in how I spent my time?
2. Which activities produced the most value?
3. What should I eliminate or delegate?
4. Suggest 3 changes for next week.
```

## The Complete Daily Flow

```
Morning (5 min): Review AI-triaged email
                   ↓
Mid-morning: Deep work (focus time)
                   ↓
Lunch: AI calendar suggests optimal afternoon
                   ↓
Meetings: AI takes notes automatically
                   ↓
End of day (5 min): AI-synthesized summary + tomorrow's priorities
```

Start with just one tool. Most people find the biggest immediate gain from AI meeting notes (Otter/Fireflies) or AI email triage. Add the next tool once you're comfortable. Within two weeks, the system will save more time than it costs.
