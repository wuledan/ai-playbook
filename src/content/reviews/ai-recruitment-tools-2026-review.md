## AI in Recruitment: Efficiency vs Fairness

AI recruitment tools promise to dramatically reduce time-to-hire while improving candidate quality. But they also raise important questions about bias, fairness, and the candidate experience. We tested three leading platforms.

## Tool Comparison

| Feature | HireVue | Ideal | Pymetrics |
|---------|:---:|:---:|:---:|

### Key Insight

These tools work best in sequence: Ideal screens CVs → Pymetrics assesses cognitive traits → HireVue conducts video interviews. Used together, they can reduce the hiring pipeline from 4 weeks to 1 week.

## FAQ

**Are AI recruitment tools biased?** All vendors claim fairness, but independent research shows bias can creep in. The best practice is to audit AI decisions regularly and never use AI as the sole decision-maker.

**Do candidates like AI interviews?** Mixed. 60% of candidates prefer the convenience of asynchronous video interviews. 40% find them impersonal. Pymetrics' gamified approach has the highest candidate satisfaction.

**Which is best for small companies?** Ideal's per-hire pricing model is most accessible. HireVue and Pymetrics typically require enterprise commitments.

**Can AI replace human recruiters?** No — but it can handle 70% of screening and scheduling work, freeing recruiters to focus on relationships and hiring decisions.
