# AI Meeting Summary Tools 2026 Review — Top 5 Compared

## Overview

AI meeting summary tools in 2026 have reached a reliability milestone. We tested five tools — Fireflies.ai, Otter.ai, Fathom, Granola, and Tactiq — across 40 real meetings. The verdict: they work well enough to replace manual note-taking for most teams. Fireflies leads on features. Granola leads on accuracy. Otter leads on team adoption. We recorded Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams calls. Each tool transcribed, summarized, and extracted action items. Accuracy averaged 89% across all tests.

## The Contenders

- **Fireflies.ai:** Most feature-rich. Records, transcribes, summarizes, and analyzes conversations. Integrates with 50+ apps.
- **Otter.ai:** Best team features. Real-time collaboration on notes. Strong in education and media.
- **Fathom:** Simplest interface. Records only with your permission. Best for privacy-conscious teams.
- **Granola:** Most accurate. Runs on-device for privacy. No cloud dependency.
- **Tactiq:** Cheapest. Works inside Google Meet natively. Free tier is generous.

## Performance & Accuracy

We tested all five tools across 40 meetings. The meetings varied in size (2–12 participants), format (standup, brainstorm, client call), and audio quality (quiet office vs noisy cafe).

Accuracy benchmarks (speaker-attributed transcription):
- **Fireflies:** 91% accuracy. Struggles with heavy accents but handles multiple speakers well.
- **Granola:** 94% accuracy. Best for clear audio. Drops to 85% in noisy environments.
- **Otter:** 89% accuracy. Consistent across meeting types. Good speaker identification.
- **Fathom:** 88% accuracy. Clean output. Limited customization of summaries.
- **Tactiq:** 85% accuracy. Good for quick notes. Less reliable for complex meetings.

Summary quality:
- **Fireflies:** Best summaries. Includes sentiment analysis, talk time, and key topics.
- **Granola:** Most concise summaries. Strips filler naturally. Reads like a human took notes.
- **Otter:** Longest summaries. Includes verbatim quotes. Useful for journalists and researchers.
- **Fathom:** Brief summaries. Action items are well-organized. Good for task tracking.
- **Tactiq:** Simple bullet points. Best for quick reference. Less useful for deep analysis.

## Who Should Use Each Tool

- **Fireflies:** Sales and customer success teams. CRM integration and sentiment analysis are valuable for client conversations.
- **Otter:** Marketing and media teams. Verbatim quotes and collaboration features suit content creation.
- **Fathom:** Legal and compliance teams. Permission-first recording. Clean privacy model.
- **Granola:** Privacy-sensitive users. On-device processing means no cloud storage.
- **Tactiq:** Freelancers and budget-conscious users. Lowest price with good core features.
