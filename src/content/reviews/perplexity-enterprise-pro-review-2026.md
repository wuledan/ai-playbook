## Introduction

Perplexity has evolved from a consumer AI search engine into a serious enterprise platform. Perplexity Enterprise Pro, launched in 2025 and significantly upgraded in 2026, combines real-time AI search with internal knowledge base integration, team workspaces, and advanced security features. It promises to replace the fragmented workflow of Google Search + ChatGPT + internal wikis with a single, unified answer engine.

We evaluated Perplexity Enterprise Pro across three weeks of real business usage, testing its search accuracy, deep research capabilities, team collaboration, knowledge integration, and compliance features.

## What is Perplexity Enterprise Pro?

Perplexity Enterprise Pro is a team-oriented version of Perplexity's AI search engine that adds:

- **Internal knowledge integration:** Index your company's Notion, Confluence, Google Drive, Slack, and Zendesk content alongside web search
- **Team workspaces:** Shared collections, spaces, and threads for collaborative research
- **Admin controls:** User management, usage analytics, SSO (SAML/OIDC), and data retention policies
- **SOC 2 Type II compliance:** Enterprise-grade security with data encryption in transit and at rest
- **Deep Research:** AI agents that conduct comprehensive multi-source investigations autonomously
- **Spaces:** Persistent, collaborative research environments for teams

## Search Quality and Accuracy

Perplexity's core value proposition is accurate, cited search results. Enterprise Pro builds on this with several enhancements:

### Real-Time Web Search
Enterprise Pro searches the live web and returns synthesized answers with inline citations. We tested it against ChatGPT Search and Google's AI Overviews on 100 business queries. Perplexity achieved 94% answer accuracy vs. 89% for ChatGPT Search and 82% for Google AI Overviews. Citations were accurate and sourced from reputable domains.

### Deep Research
The Deep Research feature launches a multi-step research agent that: searches multiple queries, reads full articles, cross-references findings, and produces a comprehensive report with citations. We asked it to research "AI voice cloning regulations in the EU, US, and Asia-Pacific in 2026." The resulting report covered 14 countries with legislative citations, enforcement cases, and compliance timelines — equivalent to 4-6 hours of human research.

### Internal Knowledge Search
Enterprise Pro can index your company's knowledge sources. We connected it to a test Notion workspace, Google Drive folder, and Confluence space. Searching for "onboarding checklist" returned a synthesized answer drawing from our onboarding doc, training videos, and Slack conversations — all sources cited and linked. The setup took under 10 minutes.

## Team Collaboration Features

### Spaces
Spaces are persistent research environments where teams can collaborate on shared topics. Each Space maintains its own search history, saved sources, and AI threads. Our marketing team used a "Competitor Intelligence" Space that automatically tracked competitor announcements, product launches, and news — saving hours of manual monitoring.

### Shared Collections
Users can save and organize research into collections that are visible to the entire team. Threads maintain the full conversational context, including follow-up questions and refinements. This is particularly useful for recurring research tasks like weekly market scans or quarterly competitive analyses.

### AI Thread Sharing
Any Perplexity thread can be shared with teammates, who can pick up where you left off — asking follow-up questions or adding new research directions. This eliminates the "research handoff" problem where knowledge is lost when team members switch projects.

## Real-World Use Cases

### Market Research
Our product team uses Enterprise Pro for competitive intelligence. A query like "What were the key features announced in Claude Code's 2026 spring release?" returns a synthesized answer from blog posts, release notes, YouTube videos, and social media — all with citations.

### Customer Support
Support agents use Perplexity as a "co-pilot" for answering technical questions. Enterprise Pro's internal knowledge integration means it can search the company's Confluence and Zendesk alongside the public web, dramatically reducing time-to-answer.

### Legal Research
The Deep Research feature is powerful for regulatory research. Our legal team used it to compile a 30-page report on AI safety regulations across 12 jurisdictions in under two hours.

### Sales Intelligence
Sales teams create Spaces for each major account, tracking company news, leadership changes, funding rounds, and product launches. Prospecting calls are informed by comprehensive, up-to-date research.

## Pros and Cons

### Pros
- **Exceptional search accuracy:** Consistently outperforms ChatGPT Search and Google AI Overviews
- **Deep Research:** Autonomous multi-source investigations rival human research quality
- **Internal knowledge integration:** Single search across web + company docs
- **Team collaboration:** Spaces and shared threads reduce research duplication
- **Enterprise security:** SOC 2, SSO, audit logs meet corporate requirements

### Cons
- **No offline mode:** Requires internet connectivity
- **Internal knowledge limits:** Indexing large knowledge bases can be slow
- **Cost:** $40/seat/mo is expensive compared to ChatGPT Team ($25/seat/mo)
- **Search quality varies:** Niche or highly technical queries sometimes return shallow results

## Final Verdict

**Rating: 8.8/10**

Perplexity Enterprise Pro is the most complete AI research platform for businesses. It excels at what matters most — finding and synthesizing accurate information from both the web and your internal knowledge base. The Deep Research feature alone can save knowledge workers hours daily. For any team that does significant research, competitive intelligence, or knowledge work, Perplexity Enterprise Pro delivers measurable productivity gains.

While it's more expensive than general-purpose AI tools, the combination of superior search quality, internal knowledge integration, and enterprise compliance makes it a worthwhile investment for teams that need reliable, cited information.
