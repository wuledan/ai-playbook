## FAQ

**Can AI replace UX researchers?** No — AI handles transcription, tagging, and pattern identification. Human researchers still design studies, ask the right questions, and interpret findings in context.

**Which is best for solo researchers?** Dovetail — most powerful AI features, good solo pricing. Condens is a close second.

**Which is best for research teams?** UserZoom — complete research operations platform from recruiting to reporting. Expensive but comprehensive.

**How accurate is AI auto-tagging?** Dovetail's auto-tagging is 80-85% accurate for common UX themes. Expect to review and adjust 15-20% of tags.
