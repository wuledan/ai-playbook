# Best AI Logo Generators 2026: Looka vs LogoAI vs Canva

A strong logo is the foundation of any brand identity, and AI logo generators have made professional-quality design accessible to everyone. But not all tools are created equal. We spent three weeks testing Looka, LogoAI, and Canva's AI logo generator, producing over 60 logo variations across five distinct brand archetypes — from tech startups to boutique cafes — to find the best option for your needs.

## Overview

The AI logo generator market has matured significantly. What started as simple icon-and-font combinators now includes full brand kit generators, vector export suites, and AI-driven color psychology analysis. We evaluated each tool on customization depth, output quality in print and digital formats, speed of iteration, and the completeness of the brand identity package. Our test setup used identical brand prompts and style preferences across all three platforms.

## Key Features

**Looka** leads in brand identity breadth. Its AI generates not just a logo but a full brand kit — color palettes, typography pairings, social media profile images, business card templates, and even branded email signatures. The logo editor uses a sidebar-driven workflow where you select industries and visual styles, then the AI presents dozens of variations. You can lock specific elements (keep the icon, change the font) and regenerate around constraints. Vector SVG, PNG, and high-res PDF exports are included at all paid tiers.

**LogoAI** excels at quick iteration with smart templates. Its strength is the "Style Match" engine that analyzes competitor logos and brand patterns in your industry to suggest designs with appropriate visual language. The AI also offers a slogan generator and tagline suggestions, which many competitors lack. LogoAI's vector editing panel is more hands-on than Looka's, giving you direct control over anchor points, curves, and spacing. This makes it the better choice if you want deeper editing without outsourcing to a designer.

**Canva's AI Logo Generator** benefits from the broader Canva ecosystem. You start with text prompts ("modern fintech logo, geometric, navy and gold"), and the AI generates 15–20 options. What sets Canva apart is that every generated logo lands inside the full Canva editor — you can instantly drop it into a pitch deck, website mockup, or social media post without exporting. The AI understands contextual constraints (horizontal vs vertical layouts, dark vs light backgrounds) reasonably well. However, custom vector export requires a Pro subscription.

## Pricing

| Basic | $20/mo — full brand kit, vector exports | $15/mo — SVG/PNG, slogan generator | $13/mo (Pro) — full exports |

Looka offers a one-time purchase option ($65–$99 per brand kit) that many freelancers prefer over subscription fatigue. LogoAI's $15/mo plan hits the sweetest value point for individuals. Canva requires a Pro subscription for commercial-use logo exports, which adds up if you only need a logo.

## Performance & Limits

**Speed**: Looka generates in 5–10 seconds, Canva in 8–15 seconds, LogoAI in 12–20 seconds (more on-brand analysis happening under the hood). All are fast enough for real-time iteration.

**Export Quality**: Looka delivers the cleanest vector files with properly grouped layers and named objects. LogoAI's SVGs sometimes produce stray anchor points on exported text curves. Canva's SVG export dissociates complex grouped elements — fine for screen use, not ideal for print production.

**Brand Consistency**: Looka's full kit (colors, fonts, templates) maintains the strictest consistency across assets. LogoAI's kit is good but limited to basic matching. Canva's AI logo sits within its design system, so consistency extends to any Canva project — but standalone use outside Canva requires manual matching.

## Who Should Use It

- **Solopreneurs & freelancers**: LogoAI — best value, solid vector control, and slogan generation built in
- **Agencies building multiple brands**: Looka — best brand kit consistency and export quality across projects
- **Marketing teams already in Canva**: Canva AI Logo — seamless integration with existing workflows
- **Print-heavy businesses**: Looka — cleanest vector output for signage, packaging, and merch
- **Quick one-off logos**: Any of the three will work, but LogoAI's $15/mo tier costs the least

## Final Verdict

Looka earns the top spot for serious brand building — its full kit approach and print-ready exports justify the premium price. Canva is the winner for teams already living inside the Canva ecosystem. LogoAI offers the best raw value for individuals who want decent vector editing without subscriptions creeping past $15/mo. All three have free trials — run the same brand prompt through each before committing.
