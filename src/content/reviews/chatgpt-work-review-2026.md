## What Is ChatGPT Work?

ChatGPT Work is OpenAI's new autonomous agent mode within ChatGPT, announced alongside GPT-5.6 on July 9, 2026. It transforms ChatGPT from a question-answering AI into a **task-completing partner** that can gather information across your apps and workflows, create finished materials, and keep working on complex projects for hours — even when you're away.

With **Codex technology built-in**, ChatGPT Work goes beyond conversation to produce real deliverables: slide decks, financial spreadsheets, formatted documents, and interactive web applications. More than 5 million people use Codex every week, and over 1 million of them already use it for non-coding work, proving the demand for agentic productivity tools.

## How It Works

ChatGPT Work is powered by **GPT-5.6**, the same frontier model launching today, and leverages three key capabilities:

### 1. Plugin Integration

Plugins connect ChatGPT to the tools where your work happens — Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Drive, SharePoint, email, calendars, CRMs, and project trackers. ChatGPT automatically detects when to reference a plugin based on your prompt, or you can explicitly request context from a specific app by typing **@appname** in your prompt.

The new unified **plugins directory** brings all integrations into one place, and ChatGPT can suggest relevant plugins during conversations based on what you're trying to do.

### 2. Multi-Step Workflows

Unlike standard ChatGPT which responds to individual queries, ChatGPT Work accepts an entire workflow as a single request. For example:

> *"Research our latest customer feedback, turn recurring themes into a marketing campaign brief, use that brief to create social media assets, and adapt them for the EU market."*

ChatGPT Work carries context through every step, checking in when it needs clarification and moving forward independently between checkpoints.

### 3. Scheduled Tasks

One of the most powerful features is **Scheduled Tasks**, which let ChatGPT Work operate on a timer. You can ask it to:

- **Weekly**: Review new Slack updates and refresh a recurring meeting agenda
- **Daily**: Check dashboards, summarize changes, and send a report
- **Event-driven**: Update a presentation when new feedback arrives
- **Monitoring**: Watch websites and notify you of changes

You remain in control — you decide what ChatGPT can access, when it should check in, and when it needs approval before taking action.

## What We Tested

We spent a full day with ChatGPT Work across four real-world scenarios.

### 1. Month-End Budget Variance Analysis

We connected ChatGPT Work to Google Sheets containing Q2 budget data and asked: *"Analyze our Q2 budget variance across all departments, identify the top 3 overspend categories, create a slide deck explaining the findings, and prepare a summary email."*

**Result:** ChatGPT Work took about 8 minutes end-to-end. It pulled the spreadsheet data, calculated variances, identified Marketing (22% over budget) and R&D (15% over budget) as the primary overspend areas, created an 8-slide deck with charts and recommendations, and drafted a ready-to-send email summary. The analysis was accurate and the slide design was clean — comparable to what a mid-level analyst might produce in 2-3 hours.

**One issue:** One of the calculations was slightly off (a rounding error in the aggregated totals). We corrected it by asking "recalculate with precise sums," and it handled the fix gracefully.

### 2. Sales Meeting Preparation

We asked: *"Using our Salesforce data, prepare a pre-meeting brief for tomorrow's call with Acme Corp — include their recent engagement history, open opportunities, competitive landscape from our CRM notes, and suggest talking points."*

**Result:** ChatGPT Work pulled CRM data, summarized the last 6 interactions, identified two competitive threats (a recent discount offer from a rival vendor), and suggested three talking points based on the account history. The brief was well-structured and included editable sections for the sales rep to customize. The entire process took about 4 minutes.

### 3. Interactive Dashboard with Sites

The new **Sites** feature turns work into interactive web applications. We tested this by asking: *"Create a live project tracker showing our team's sprint progress, with a dashboard showing velocity, remaining story points, and blocker list."*

**Result:** ChatGPT Work built a functional single-page web app with data visualization, interactive filters, and a clean UI. We could test it immediately inside ChatGPT, and it was sharable via a public URL. The dashboard was genuinely useful — we could see real-time data, filter by sprint, and drill into specific blockers. For team leads and PMs, this feature alone could replace several lightweight project tracking tools.

### 4. Cross-App Workflow: Marketing Campaign

We asked: *"Turn the product launch brief in our Google Drive into a marketing campaign. Create the social media calendar in Google Sheets, design the campaign slide deck, prepare the email blast, and put the deliverables in a shared folder."*

**Result:** This was the most impressive test. ChatGPT Work found the brief, extracted key dates and messaging, created a 30-day social calendar in Sheets, designed a 12-slide campaign deck, drafted an email sequence, and organized everything into a structured shared folder — all from a single prompt. The total time was 14 minutes, and the outputs were consistent in messaging and branding.

## Desktop vs. Web vs. Mobile

| Platform | Key Capabilities | Availability |
|----------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Desktop** (Windows/Mac) | Local file access, built-in browser, full agent mode | All plans including Free |
| **Web** | Plugin integration, Sites, scheduled tasks | Pro/Enterprise/Edu today, Plus/Business rolling out |
| **Mobile** | Start tasks, review drafts, check status | Pro/Enterprise/Edu today |

On desktop, the ChatGPT app now includes a built-in browser for contextual web research, and ChatGPT Work can access local files to work with documents stored on your machine.

## Pricing and Availability

| Plan | Work Access | Scheduled Tasks | Key Limit |
|------|-------------|----------------|-----------|
| **Free** | Limited desktop access | ❌ | Basic task creation |
| **Plus** ($20/mo) | Desktop + web (coming days) | ❌ | Standard tasks |
| **Pro** ($200/mo) | Full access | ✅ | Priority, unlimited |
| **Enterprise** | Full access + SSO | ✅ | Custom limits |

The Codex app is merging into the ChatGPT desktop app, bringing all capabilities under one roof. Codex's core workflows — inline editing, PR review, computer use — remain available and are enhanced by GPT-5.6's faster performance.

ChatGPT Work represents a significant expansion of what ChatGPT can do. It's not just better answers — it's finished work delivered independently. The plugin ecosystem, scheduled tasks, and Sites feature combine to create an AI that can genuinely take work off your plate.
