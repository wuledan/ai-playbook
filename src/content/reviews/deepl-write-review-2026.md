## Quick Verdict

DeepL Write occupies a unique niche in the AI writing assistant market: it's the only tool that genuinely excels at multilingual writing refinement. Where Grammarly feels like an English-first tool with other languages bolted on, DeepL Write was built from the ground up to handle German, French, Spanish, Dutch, Italian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, and Japanese with the same level of sophistication as English.

In my testing, DeepL Write's German-to-English suggestions were noticeably more idiomatic than Grammarly's, which often produced grammatically correct but stilted translations. The tone sliders — Casual, Business, Formal — offer real granularity, letting you dial in exactly how authoritative or conversational you want your writing to sound.

**The catch:** It's a refinement tool, not a full writing platform. No browser extension. No generative AI drafting. No plagiarism checks. No integrations with the apps you actually write in (Google Docs, Outlook, Slack). You have to copy-paste text to the web app, refine it, and copy-paste back. That friction matters.

**Our rating: 8.2/10** — best-in-class multilingual refinement, but limited scope.

### Tone Sliders

Instead of AI-driven tone detection, DeepL Write offers three manual tone sliders:

- **Casual → Business → Formal**: Adjusts vocabulary and sentence structure. At Casual, contractions and simpler words are preferred. At Formal, the tool prefers longer words and complex structures.
- **Gender**: Choose masculine, feminine, or neutral forms (critical for German, French, Spanish)
- **Tone Profile**: Select from Default, Diplomatic, Enthusiastic, Informative, and more

In practice, the tone sliders are effective but require manual intervention. There's no automated tone detection — DeepL won't tell you "this sentence sounds hostile," the way Grammarly does.

### Word and Phrase Alternatives

This is DeepL Write's standout feature. For any given sentence, it offers multiple alternative phrasings with bullet-point clarity:

**Original:** "We need to talk about the project deadline."

**DeepL Alternatives:**
- "We need to discuss the project timeline." (Business)
- "We should touch base on the project deadline." (Casual)
- "I require a discussion regarding the project deadline." (Formal)

Each alternative is genuinely different in tone and structure, not just a minor word swap.

### Translation-Integrated Workflow

Since DeepL owns both the translation engine and Write, the integration is seamless. You can:
1. Draft in your native language
2. Translate to English with DeepL Translator
3. Open the translated text in DeepL Write for refinement
4. Use tone sliders to adjust the voice

This pipeline is particularly powerful for non-native English speakers writing for international audiences.

### German-to-English Refinement

**Input:** A 500-word German business email translated through Google Translate.

**DeepL Write's refinements:** Caught 16 awkward constructions that stemmed from literal translation — false cognates, incorrect prepositions, unnatural word order. The Business tone setting produced a compelling, professional English email that read as native-written.

**Grammarly's refinements:** Caught 4 surface-level issues but missed the deeper structural awkwardness.

## Alternatives {#alternatives}

- **[Grammarly](https://grammarly.com)**: Better English grammar checking (95% vs 81%), browser extension everywhere, tone detection, and generative AI with GrammarlyGO. Weaker multilingual support. $12/mo Pro.

- **[ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com)**: In-depth style analysis with 25+ reports including overused words, sticky sentences, and pacing. Better for long-form writing like books. $15/mo.

- **[Wordtune](https://wordtune.com)**: Focuses specifically on sentence rewriting and tone adjustment. Has a browser extension. Less language variety but better English rewrite quality than DeepL Write in casual contexts. $10/mo.

## FAQ {#faq}

### Is DeepL Write free?
DeepL Write has a Free tier limited to 2,000 characters per refinement and a daily refinement cap. For occasional use it's fine, but for regular writing work the Pro Starter (€8.99/mo) or Pro Advanced (€27.49/mo) plans are necessary.

### Does DeepL Write have a browser extension?
No. This is one of the biggest limitations. DeepL Write is a web app only — you paste text in, refine it, and paste it back to your destination. There are no Chrome extensions, Google Docs add-ins, or desktop apps. DeepL Translator does have browser extensions and desktop apps, but Write does not.

### Can DeepL Write write new content from scratch?
No. Unlike GrammarlyGO or Jasper, DeepL Write cannot generate new content. It only refines and improves text you provide. If you need AI drafting, you'll need to pair DeepL Write with ChatGPT, Jasper, or Copy.ai.

### How does DeepL Write handle different English varieties?
DeepL Write supports US, UK, Australian, and Canadian English. The differences are small but present — UK English gets British spelling (colour, organise) and vocabulary preferences (flat vs apartment, lift vs elevator). The formality sliders work across all variants.

### Is DeepL Write good for academic writing?
It depends on the language. For English academic writing, ProWritingAid is a better choice with dedicated academic style checks. For multilingual academic writing (e.g., writing a German-language paper with English abstract), DeepL Write's ability to handle both languages natively is a significant advantage.

### Does DeepL Write work offline?
No. All refinement is processed on DeepL's servers. An internet connection is required. There is no offline mode.
