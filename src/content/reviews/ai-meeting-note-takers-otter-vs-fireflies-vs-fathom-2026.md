# AI Meeting Note Takers 2026: Otter vs Fireflies vs Fathom vs Granola

AI meeting note-takers have become essential productivity tools for teams drowning in back-to-back Zoom calls. We put Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, Fathom, and Granola through rigorous testing across transcription accuracy, action item extraction, integration quality, and real-world meeting scenarios.

## Overview

The AI meeting note-taker market has exploded in 2026. Each tool takes a fundamentally different approach. Otter.ai is the veteran with deep Zoom-native integration and its own OtterPilot assistant. Fireflies.ai focuses on searchability and team-wide deployment. Fathom positions itself as the lightweight, always-on companion that works everywhere. Granola takes a unique hybrid approach, combining AI transcription with manual note-taking for maximum accuracy. We tested all four over 40+ hours of real and simulated meetings.

## Pricing

**Otter.ai** — Free tier (300 mins/month), Pro $16.99/mo (1,200 mins), Business $30/user/mo (6,000 mins). The free tier is surprisingly usable for light users.

**Fireflies.ai** — Free tier (800 mins, limited search), Pro $19/mo (unlimited storage), Business $29/user/mo (unlimited + analytics). Best per-minute value at scale.

**Fathom** — Free tier (unlimited recordings with watermark), Pro $19/mo (unlimited, no watermark, advanced AI), Teams $29/user/mo. Standout for offering unlimited free recording.

**Granola** — Free tier (5 hours/month), Pro $20/mo (unlimited), Enterprise custom pricing. The most expensive option per user but offers unique value with its hybrid note-taking approach.

## Performance & Limits

We tested each tool across five meeting types: one-on-one standup, team planning, client presentation, technical discussion, and a chaotic 8-person brainstorming session.

**Transcription accuracy** averaged 94-97% across all tools under ideal conditions. Fathom and Otter tied at 97% in quiet rooms. In noisy environments with overlapping speakers, Fireflies dropped to 91% while Granola's hybrid approach maintained 95% by letting the human correct in real-time.

**Action item extraction** was Fireflies' strongest feature — it captured 88% of explicit action items vs 72% for Otter and 78% for Fathom. Granola's hybrid model produced near-perfect extraction but required active participation from the note-taker.

**Latency** — Otter and Fathom delivered transcripts in near real-time (< 2 seconds). Fireflies had a 3-5 second delay. Granola presented notes after the meeting ended, with AI refinement taking 30-60 seconds.

## Comparison / Alternatives

**Fathom vs Otter.ai** — Fathom's unlimited free tier makes it the easy choice for individuals. Otter's OtterPilot and deeper Zoom integration favor teams already deep in the Zoom ecosystem.

**Fireflies vs Granola** — Fireflies is best for teams that need post-meeting search across hundreds of past conversations. Granola is ideal for power note-takers who want AI assistance without losing manual control.

**Alternatives not tested:** Krisp (focuses on noise cancellation, not note-taking), Sembly AI (good for enterprise governance), and tl;dv (strong for video timestamped notes with async communication teams).

## Who Should Use It

- **Individual professionals and freelancers** — Start with Fathom's free tier. Zero cost, excellent transcription, unlimited recordings.
- **Small to medium teams (5-50 people)** — Fireflies.ai offers the best team-wide search and CRM integration. The searchability alone can save hours per week.
- **Enterprise teams (50+)** — Granola's desktop-first approach with offline capability and human-AI hybrid model suits compliance-heavy environments.
- **Sales teams** — Fireflies wins with 60+ CRM integrations and automated call logging.
- **Product and engineering teams** — Fathom's lightweight approach works well for daily standups and technical discussions where deep CRM integration isn't needed.

## Final Verdict

No single tool dominates across every dimension. **Fathom** earns our top recommendation for individuals and small teams thanks to its generous free tier and excellent accuracy. **Fireflies.ai** is the best choice for team-wide deployment, particularly for sales and customer-facing teams that need CRM integration and meeting search. **Otter.ai** remains a strong contender for Zoom-first organizations. **Granola** occupies a unique niche for those who want AI assistance without sacrificing manual control.

The category is evolving fast. By mid-2026, expect deeper cross-platform integration (especially Microsoft Teams) and improved action item extraction as foundation models continue to improve. For now, pick based on your team size and primary communication platform.
