## Quick Verdict

Recraft has carved a unique position in the AI image generation landscape by focusing on what most other tools ignore: **brand consistency and vector output.** While Midjourney creates stunning one-off art and DALL·E excels at creative interpretation, Recraft is built for the messy reality of commercial design — where every asset needs to look like it belongs to the same brand, and where SVG files are more useful than PNGs.

After using Recraft daily for two weeks to generate brand assets — logos, icons, social media graphics, product mockups, and vector illustrations — we rate it **8.0/10**. The brand kit system, vector output capability, and all-in-one tool suite make it the most practical AI design platform for brand teams. The V4.1 model update brought significant improvements in prompt understanding, composition, and text generation.

**Verdict**: The best AI design tool for brand-consistent asset production. For one-off artistic images or high-end photorealism, look elsewhere. For everything a brand team needs to produce daily — Recraft is the tool you want.

## Detailed Feature Analysis

### Vector Generation (Editable SVG Output)

This is Recraft's headline feature and the main reason designers choose it over Midjourney or DALL·E. Recraft can generate:

- **Flat vector illustrations:** Clean, scalable graphics for websites and apps
- **Icons and pictograms:** Consistent iconography in your brand style
- **Logos and wordmarks:** Simple to moderately complex logo concepts
- **Infographic elements:** Charts, diagrams, and data visualization components

The vector output comes as editable SVG files that can be opened in Illustrator, Figma, or Affinity Designer. You can modify colors, resize to any dimension, adjust paths, and combine elements — things impossible with raster images from other AI generators.

In practice, complex vectors (multi-element compositions, detailed characters) require cleanup. Strokes may be grouped differently than expected, and nested elements sometimes need reorganization. But for simple-to-moderate vectors — icons, buttons, simple illustrations — the output is production-ready with minimal editing.

### Brand Kit System

Recraft's brand kit is a game-changer for teams producing consistent visual assets. You define:

- **Color palette:** Up to 10 brand colors with hex codes
- **Typography:** Brand fonts for any text generated inside images
- **Visual style:** Upload reference images (2–5) to define style, composition, and mood
- **Logo lockup:** Add your logo for watermark placement

Once configured, every generation — whether an Instagram post, a banner ad, or an icon set — automatically applies the brand kit. This eliminates the "different-generations-don't-match" problem that plagues every other AI tool. In testing, brand kit consistency was impressive — across 50 generations of different asset types, color accuracy was nearly perfect, and style similarity rated over 85% in blind testing.

### Style Control via Reference Images

You can upload 2–5 reference images to define a visual style — "make everything look like this." This is more powerful than Midjourney's style reference because Recraft's model is explicitly trained to extract and apply consistent style features. In practice, this means:

- Upload a brand deck → generate 100 on-brand social graphics
- Upload a product photo → generate matching lifestyle imagery
- Upload an illustration style → generate a full icon set in that style

### Text-in-Image Generation

Recraft V4.1 significantly improved text rendering. It now rivals Ideogram for short text (5–8 characters) and surpasses Midjourney and Stable Diffusion. Brand text (company names, taglines, headers) in generated images is typically readable, though long strings and complex typography still fail occasionally.

## Pros & Cons (Expanded)

**Pros:**

**Vector output is a genuine differentiator.** No other AI image tool generates production-ready SVG files. For designers who need icons, logos, and illustrations that scale to any size, this is transformative.

**Brand kit solves the consistency problem.** The single biggest challenge with AI-generated visual assets is that they don't look like they come from the same brand. Recraft's brand kit is the only real solution to this problem on the market.

**All-in-one tool suite.** Image generator, vector generator, photo editor, background remover, upscaler, eraser, mockup generator — it's a complete design toolkit, not a single-purpose generator.

**Cons:**

**Photorealism gap.** Natural images — portraits, landscapes, product photography — don't match Midjourney or DALL·E quality. Faces can look artificial, lighting physics sometimes breaks down, and textures lack the subtle detail of leader models.

**Complex vectors need cleanup.** Multi-element vector illustrations require manual reorganization in Illustrator or Figma. Layers come in arbitrary groupings, and complex path structures occasionally have artifacts.

**Free plan limitations.** Free-tier images are owned by Recraft and publicly visible. For any commercial use, a paid plan is effectively required. The 100 monthly credits on Free are also very restrictive.

## Who Should Use This (and Who Shouldn't)

**Who should use Recraft:** Brand and marketing teams producing high-volume social media assets. Small business owners maintaining brand consistency without a full design team. Product designers needing consistent icons and illustrations. E-commerce sellers creating product mockups, ad creative, and packaging visuals.

**Who should skip Recraft:** Photographers and digital artists needing photorealistic output. Users who only need one-off images and don't care about brand consistency. Designers who prefer working in established tools like Figma or Canva for their entire workflow. Anyone on a tight budget who can't justify the Basic plan.

## FAQ

**Can I sell designs made with Recraft?**
Yes, on paid plans. Free plan images are owned by Recraft and can only be used commercially with limitations. Paid plan images grant full commercial rights and are private.

**Does Recraft support PNG or only SVG?**
Both. Recraft generates PNG for raster images and SVG for vectors. You can also download JPEG and WebP formats.

**How do brand kits work?**
Upload 2–5 reference images (brand guidelines, existing assets, mood boards). Recraft extracts color palette, visual style, composition preferences, and texture patterns. Every generation thereafter maintains that style. You can create multiple brand kits and switch between them.

**Is there a Recraft mobile app?**
Not currently. Recraft is web-only, but the interface is responsive and works on tablets.

**Can I train a custom model on Recraft?**
Not directly. Brand kit and style control achieve consistent output without model training. The Enterprise plan includes custom model fine-tuning options.
