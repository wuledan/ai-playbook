## FAQ

**Can AI replace contract lawyers?** No — AI handles first-pass review, clause extraction, and risk flagging. Human lawyers handle nuanced negotiation, unusual clauses, and strategic advice. The best use is AI as a force multiplier.

**Which is best for small legal teams?** Evisort offers the most accessible pricing for mid-market companies. Lexion has the fastest implementation. Ironclad is best for enterprises with existing CLM needs.

**How accurate is AI clause detection?** 82-88% for standard clauses. For unusual or highly customized contracts, accuracy drops. Always have a human review AI-flagged risks.

**Do these tools support non-English contracts?** Ironclad and Lexion support English primarily, with limited French and German. Evisort has better international language support.
