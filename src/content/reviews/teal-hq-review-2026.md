## Quick Verdict

Teal HQ positions itself as an "AI career operating system" — a central hub for managing your entire job search from resume optimization through application tracking. The ambition is admirable, and the resume builder with ATS keyword analysis is genuinely useful. The job tracker with its kanban board gives you a clear picture of where each application stands, which is invaluable when you're juggling 15+ simultaneous applications.

I used Teal for 6 weeks during an active job search — targeting product management roles at mid-size tech companies. The ATS keyword matching feature alone was worth the subscription: I optimized my resume for each of 12 applications, and the keyword gap analysis caught missing terms that were literally on the first page of three job descriptions I had been submitting unmodified.

**The catch:** The price. At $18/mo (Plus) or $55/mo (Premium), Teal is expensive for what it does. The free tier is too limited to be useful beyond evaluating the product. And the AI features, while helpful, aren't doing anything you couldn't do manually with a checklist and ChatGPT — they just make it faster.

**Our rating: 7.6/10** — a genuinely helpful job search companion that needs either a lower price or deeper features to justify the premium.

## Features & Capabilities {#features}

### AI Resume Builder

The core feature. Teal parses your existing resume (PDF or DOCX) into editable sections, then provides AI-powered optimization tools:

| Feature | What It Does | Free | Plus | Premium |
|---------|-------------|------|------|---------|
| Resume parsing | Auto-extracts sections from existing resume | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Bullet optimizer | Rewrites experience bullets for impact | ❌ | ✅ (50/mo) | ✅ (Unlimited) |
| ATS keyword analysis | Scans job listings and compares to your resume | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Resume scoring | Auto-ranks resume against job description | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Multiple resume profiles | Different versions for different job types | ❌ | ✅ (3) | ✅ (Unlimited) |
| Design templates | Professionally designed layouts | ✅ (1) | ✅ (5) | ✅ (10+) |

### Bullet Optimization in Practice

**Original bullet:** "Managed a team of 5 engineers building the payment system."

**Teal AI rewrite:** "Led a cross-functional team of 5 engineers to architect and deploy a scalable payment processing system, reducing transaction failures by 40% and achieving PCI-DSS compliance ahead of the regulatory deadline."

The AI adds context, results, and keywords. The quality varies — when your original bullet lacks data, the AI invents estimates (which you should verify and adjust). The formula is effective: Action verb + what you did + how you did it + measurable result.

### Job Application Tracker

A kanban board with 6 default columns:
- **Saved** — Jobs bookmarked for later
- **Applied** — Applications submitted
- **Phone Screen** — First-round interviews
- **Interview** — In-progress interviews
- **Offer** — Offers received
- **Rejected** — Self-explanatory
- **Archived** — No longer pursuing

For each application, you can track:
- Job title, company, and link to posting
- Date applied
- Salary range (publicly listed or your estimate)
- Contacts at the company
- Notes and next steps
- Resume version used for this application

### Cover Letter Generator

Generates a tailored cover letter based on your resume and the specific job description. Output is 300-400 words, professional tone. Quality is similar to ChatGPT with a good prompt — 70% usable, 30% needs editing for personal voice.

### LinkedIn Optimization

Teal scans your LinkedIn profile and generates an optimization report:
- **Headline:** Does it contain recruiter-searchable keywords?
- **Summary:** Is it compelling and keyword-rich?
- **Experience:** Are the bullet points as strong as your resume version?
- **Recommendations:** Specific rewrites for each section

## Hands-On Testing

### Test: Managing 12 Simultaneous Applications

**Setup:** Applied to 12 product management roles over 3 weeks. Used Teal to track all applications and optimize resumes for each.

**Results:**
| Metric | Before Teal | With Teal |
|--------|-------------|-----------|
| Applications tracked | Scattered across email/notes | Centralized kanban |
| Resume versions managed | 1 generic version | 12 optimized versions |
| Cover letters | Generic for all | Tailored per posting |
| Interview rate | 1/10 applications | 3/12 applications |
| Time per application | 45 minutes | 30 minutes (+10 min resume opt) |

**The 3x interview rate isn't purely from resume optimization — I was also applying more selectively and to better-fitting roles. But the ATS keyword analysis definitely helped for two applications where I was missing "stakeholder management" and "OKR planning" from my resume.

### Resume Score Feature

Teal scores your resume against a specific job description on a 0-100 scale:
- **Content match:** How well your experience matches the JD
- **Keyword coverage:** What percentage of JD keywords are in your resume
- **Formatting:** ATS readability assessment

My base resume scored 62/100 against a typical PM job. After Teal optimization, I improved to 81/100. The score is somewhat gamified — you can chase 100% keyword coverage but it leads to keyword stuffing. The best use is identifying critical missing terms, not maximizing the score.

## Pros & Cons {#pros-cons}

### Pros 👍
- **ATS keyword analysis is genuinely useful** — identifies gaps you miss when skimming job descriptions
- **Bullet optimizer produces stronger experience descriptions** — the result-inclusive format is effective for recruiter review
- **Job tracker provides clarity** — seeing all applications in one kanban board reduces search anxiety
- **Cover letter generation saves 15-20 minutes per application** — needs editing but good starting point
- **LinkedIn optimization** catches recruiter search visibility issues

### Cons 👎
- **Expensive** — $216/yr for Plus, $432/yr for Premium is a lot for job search software
- **Annual-only subscription commitment** — no monthly option for Plus/Premium
- **AI suggestions can feel templated** — bullet rewrites follow the same formula
- **No interview preparation or negotiation tools** — the career journey coverage is incomplete
- **Job search tool for active periods only** — not a continuous career management platform

## Alternatives {#alternatives}

- **[Rezi](https://rezi.ai)**: AI resume builder focused on ATS optimization with real-time keyword targeting. More affordable at $7.99/mo (annual). Stronger ATS scoring algorithm. Weaker job tracker.

- **[Enhancv](https://enhancv.com)**: Professional resume builder with modern design templates. Better for visual resume design. Good LinkedIn profile export. Less AI optimization. $12/mo (annual).

- **[Simplify](https://simplify.jobs)**: Free browser extension that auto-fills job applications for 200,000+ companies. Includes job board aggregation and salary insights. Less resume optimization but better application speed. Free.

- **[ChatGPT + a spreadsheet](https://chatgpt.com)**: Use ChatGPT to optimize resume bullets ($20/mo) and a spreadsheet or Notion for tracking. Less integrated but much cheaper. Full control over AI prompts and tracking structure.

## FAQ {#faq}

### Is Teal HQ free?
Teal has a Free plan that includes 1 resume profile and basic job tracking. The Plus plan ($18/mo annual) is needed for ATS analysis, resume scoring, and bullet optimization. Premium ($36/mo annual) adds unlimited optimization and custom templates.

### Does Teal actually help get more interviews?
In my testing, the interview rate increased from 10% to 25%. The ATS keyword optimization was the primary driver — particularly for roles in competitive fields where matching specific jargon matters. However, improved targeting (applying to better-fitting roles) was also a factor.

### Can Teal fill out job applications automatically?
No — this is a common misconception. Simplify (separate product) does auto-fill. Teal is a management and optimization platform: you still need to submit applications manually. Teal helps you prepare the resume and cover letter, and track the application.

### Is Teal only for tech professionals?
No, but it's strongest for tech and business roles. The resume templates and AI optimization work across industries. However, the job tracker's column structure (Applied → Phone Screen → Interview → Offer) assumes a corporate hiring pipeline common in tech. Creative and trade industries with less structured hiring processes may find the model less fitting.

### Can I use Teal without giving it my LinkedIn credentials?
Yes. LinkedIn optimization can be done by pasting your profile text — no OAuth login required for any core feature. The job tracker is fully manual and doesn't scrape LinkedIn or job boards.
