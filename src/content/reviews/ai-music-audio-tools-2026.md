## The Sound of AI

AI music generation has exploded in capability. In 2026, you can generate a complete song from a text prompt — lyrics, vocals, instrumentation, and production — in under 60 seconds. We tested the four leading platforms to find out which one makes the best noise.

## Suno 4: Full Song Generation Leader

Suno remains the frontrunner for end-to-end music generation. Version 4 introduced "StyleDNA" — a feature that analyzes existing songs and applies their sonic characteristics to new compositions. The vocal quality has improved dramatically, with almost no "AI warble."

**Strengths:** Best full-song output, improved vocals, large community
**Weaknesses:** Hit-or-miss lyrics, limited fine-tuning
**Price:** Free (5 songs/day), $10/mo (100 songs), $30/mo (unlimited)

## Udio: The Musician's Sandbox

Udio positions itself as a music production tool, not just a song generator. Its standout feature is "Remix Mode" — you can generate a track, then remix specific elements (change the bass line, swap the drum pattern, add a bridge).

**Strengths:** Best remixing tools, widest genre library, stem export
**Weaknesses:** Slower generation, less polished initial outputs
**Price:** Free (10 songs/mo), $15/mo (500 songs), $30/mo (unlimited)

## ElevenLabs Music: Voice + Music

ElevenLabs entered music generation by leveraging their best-in-class voice synthesis. Their music platform integrates voice cloning with music generation, making it ideal for podcast intros, audiobook scoring, and branded audio content.

**Strengths:** Best voice quality, music+voice integration, sound effects
**Weaknesses:** Limited music-only generation, fewer genres
**Price:** $5/mo (Starter), $22/mo (Creator), $99/mo (Pro)

## Soundraw: Royalty-Free Done Right

Soundraw is different — it's a royalty-free music library augmented by AI. You start with AI-generated tracks, then customize length, intensity, instrumentation, and structure. Everything is royalty-free and cleared for commercial use.

**Strengths:** 100% royalty-free, commercial-safe, easy customization
**Weaknesses:** Less creative freedom, can't generate completely original compositions
**Price:** $17/mo (Creator), $30/mo (Unlimited)

## Use Case Recommendations

- **Podcast intros & outros:** ElevenLabs Music
- **Background music for video:** Soundraw
- **Full song generation:** Suno 4
- **Music production & remixing:** Udio
- **Voiceovers for video:** ElevenLabs
- **Commercial music libraries:** Soundraw

## The Legal Landscape

Important caveat: AI music copyright is unsettled. Major labels have sued several AI music platforms. For commercial projects, **Soundraw** and **Suno's paid tier** offer the clearest licensing terms. Always check the latest terms before using AI-generated music commercially.

## Verdict

For most creators, **Suno 4** is the best starting point — it's the most impressive showcase of what's possible. For serious production work, combine **Udio** for music generation with **ElevenLabs** for voice work. And for commercial-safe background music, **Soundraw** is the only clear choice.
