## Real-World Testing: 50 Use Cases Over 8 Weeks

### Document Analysis: 500-Page Technical Manual

We uploaded a 500-page PDF of a Kubernetes administration guide:

- **Upload time:** 22 seconds
- **Query:** "What are the specific steps to configure etcd backup on page 423?"
- **Response time:** 6 seconds (with 500K+ tokens loaded)
- **Accuracy:** Correct — identified the exact section, quoted the procedure, added context from earlier sections
- **Competitor comparison:** Claude (200K context) couldn't load the full document; ChatGPT (128K) also failed

This is Gemini's killer feature. No other consumer AI handles this volume without chunking.

### Deep Research: "State of AI Coding Tools 2026"

We ran Deep Research on a current topic:

- **Time:** 7.5 minutes
- **Sources:** 14 cited (mix of news, blogs, and official docs)
- **Quality:** Good for an overview. However, it missed two niche sources we expected (a detailed Reddit comparison thread and a specific benchmark report)
- **Recommendation:** Use Deep Research for first drafts. Supplement with manual searches for niche topics.

### Coding Accuracy: Custom Benchmark

We tested Gemini 2.5 Pro against Claude 4 Opus and GPT-4.5 on 30 coding tasks:

| Model | Overall Score | Python | TypeScript | Rust | Bug Fix Rate |
|-------|-------|--------|------------|------|-------------|
| **Claude 4 Opus** | 72% | 76% | 70% | 68% | 92% |
| **GPT-4.5** | 68% | 70% | 66% | 62% | 85% |
| **Gemini 2.5 Pro** | 62% | 64% | 60% | 56% | 78% |

**Key finding:** Gemini's code looks good stylistically but has more subtle logic errors. On a React hooks task, Gemini generated clean code that compiled — but had a stale closure bug that only appeared under specific conditions. Claude caught the same edge case during generation.

## Step-by-Step: Using Deep Research for Market Analysis

Here's the exact workflow:

**Step 1:** Open Gemini → toggle "Deep Research" mode
**Step 2:** Enter query: "Analyze the current state of AI-powered coding assistants in 2026, including market leaders, pricing trends, and key differentiators"
**Step 3:** Deep Research searches the web (~3 minutes), reads sources, and generates a plan
**Step 4:** Review the plan → approve or refine the focus areas
**Step 5:** Full report generates (~5 minutes) with 12-15 cited sources
**Step 6:** Use the "double-check" button to verify key claims
**Step 7:** Export to Google Docs for editing

**Real outcome:** We got a 3,000-word research report in 8 minutes. Quality was solid for a first draft. We estimated manual research would take 2-3 hours. However, 8% of claims failed the double-check — they were based on thin sourcing rather than hallucinated, but still needed verification.
