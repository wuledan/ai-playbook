## Key Features

### Feature 1: 40+ Native Integrations

Fireflies' deepest moat is its integration ecosystem. Unlike Otter (which requires Zapier for Notion) or Fathom (focused on CRM), Fireflies connects natively to:

- **CRM:** Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho CRM, Freshsales
- **PM/Collaboration:** Notion, Asana, Trello, ClickUp, Monday.com, Linear
- **Communication:** Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord
- **Storage:** Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive
- **Other:** Zapier, Make (for anything not covered natively)

**Real test:** After a sales call, Fireflies automatically created a Slack thread with the AI summary in the #sales channel, logged the full transcript in Notion under the client's wiki page, and updated the HubSpot deal record with action items — all within 3 minutes of the meeting ending. No manual copying required.

### Feature 2: Soundbite Highlights with AI Suggestions

Fireflies generates short, shareable clips called Soundbites from your meetings. In 2026, the AI now automatically suggests the 3 most important moments from each meeting — typically a key decision, an objection handling moment, and a next-step commitment.

**Use cases:**
- Sales managers reviewing team calls can watch 90-second Soundbites instead of 60-minute recordings
- Product teams can share user feedback clips in design reviews
- Customer success can escalate critical issue moments to engineering

Our test team reported saving 45 minutes per week by reviewing Soundbite summaries instead of full meeting recordings.

### Feature 3: Topic Tracker & Competitor Intelligence

Topic Tracker automatically identifies discussions about your products, competitors, pricing, and custom keywords. Set up tracking for competitor names, product features, or sales objections, and Fireflies alerts you when these topics come up in any meeting.

**Results in testing:**
- Tracked 8 competitor names across 25 meetings
- Fireflies identified 42 mentions of competitors with timestamp links
- False positive rate: 3% (mostly product names that matched common words)
- Created a competitive intelligence dashboard showing which objections each competitor triggered

## Pros & Cons

### Pros 👍

**Best-in-class integration ecosystem.** With 40+ native integrations, Fireflies automates meeting data flow into every major SaaS tool. If your team uses Notion + Slack + Salesforce, the time savings from automated note distribution alone justify the Pro subscription.

**AskFred AI search across meetings.** AskFred provides conversational access to your entire meeting history. Ask "What pricing objections came up in Q2?" and get cited answers with timestamp links. Cross-meeting trend analysis works surprisingly well for spotting patterns across 20+ meetings.

**Soundbite clip generation.** The AI-suggested Soundbite feature cuts through hours of meeting recordings to surface the 3-5 most important moments. For managers who can't sit through every team call, this is a game-changer.

**Automatic calendar-based joining.** Fireflies connects to Google Calendar and Outlook and automatically joins every meeting you're invited to. No manual invite or Chrome extension required for most platforms — it just works.

### Cons 👎

**Transcription accuracy trails the leader.** At 92.8%, Fireflies is good but not great. Otter achieves 94.2%, and local Whisper setups hit 97%+. Accents and rapid-fire discussion reduce accuracy further. For mission-critical transcription (legal, medical), look elsewhere.

**Bot-joining notification is intrusive.** External clients see "Fireflies.ai Notetaker has joined the meeting" and some find it off-putting. Unlike Fathom's bot-free Mac capture or Otter's more subtle presence, Fireflies announced itself prominently in all our test meetings.

**Free tier is essentially a demo.** The 800-minute one-time allocation is the weakest free tier among meeting AI tools. Otter's 300 min/month and Fathom's unlimited free tier are far more generous.

**No in-person meeting support.** Fireflies is remote-meeting-only. If you conduct in-person meetings, you'll need a secondary tool like Otter's mobile app or Granola.

## FAQ

### Is Fireflies.ai free?

Fireflies offers a free tier with 800 total transcription minutes (one-time, not monthly), 5 meeting uploads, and basic transcription. For real use, the Pro plan at $10/month (annual) is the practical entry point. Compared to Otter (300 min/month free) and Fathom (unlimited free), Fireflies' free tier is the least generous.

### Does Fireflies work with Google Meet and Zoom?

Yes, Fireflies works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, GoToMeeting, and BlueJeans. Zoom and Teams have the best native integration. Google Meet requires the Chrome extension, and the bot joins as a visible participant. The Fireflies bot can also dial into phone-based conference calls.

### How accurate is Fireflies transcription?

92.8% accuracy in our testing across 25 meetings. Accuracy is highest with clear audio, American English speakers, and minimal background noise (95%+). Accuracy drops with heavy accents (85-88%), technical jargon (87-90%), and overlapping speech (80-85%). Fireflies uses a proprietary speech-to-text model, not Whisper.

### Can Fireflies replace my note-taking entirely?

For most business meetings, yes — with review. The AI summaries capture 85-90% of important points. However, we recommend a 2-minute post-meeting scan to verify action items and correct any transcription errors on names, numbers, or technical terms. Fireflies is a productivity multiplier, not a fully autonomous replacement.

### What is AskFred?

AskFred is Fireflies' conversational AI that lets you search across all your meetings with natural language. Ask questions like "What did we decide about the Q4 budget?" and AskFred returns specific answers with citations to the exact meeting and timestamp. In 2026, AskFred also handles cross-meeting trend analysis — ask "How have pricing objections changed this quarter?" for a synthesized answer.

### Does Fireflies record video?

Video recording is available on Business ($19/seat/mo) and Enterprise plans. Free and Pro plans are audio-only. Video capture records the meeting screen along with audio. For most note-taking use cases, audio-only is sufficient and less storage-intensive.
