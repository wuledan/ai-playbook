## Quick Verdict

AI presentation makers have reached parity with human-designed slides for simple decks, but still struggle with complex data and brand consistency. **Gamma** produces the most complete presentations end-to-end (content + design). **Tome** is unmatched for storytelling. **Canva AI Magic Studio** is the Swiss Army knife for visual content.

For most business users, Gamma ($10/mo) is the best value — it generates a complete, well-designed presentation from a single prompt. For creative pitches and narratives, Tome is worth the switch.

**Our pick: Gamma** — Best balance of speed, quality, and versatility.
