## Features Deep Dive

### AI Meeting Summaries

Fathom offers two levels of AI summaries:

**Chronological Summary (Free)** — A well-structured, timestamped timeline of the meeting with logical section breaks. Good for quick review. Accuracy: 85-90% for standard business meetings.

**Advanced Summary (Premium/Team/Business)** — This is where Fathom shines. Choose from 15+ expert frameworks:

| Template | Best For | Example Output |
|---|---|---|
| BANT | Sales qualifying | Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline assessment |
| Sandler | Sales discovery | Pain points, budget, decision process |
| MEDDIC | Enterprise sales | Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria |
| Standard Business | General meetings | Decisions, action items, next steps |
| Product Feedback | User research | Feature requests, pain points, sentiment |
| Support Call | Customer support | Issue description, resolution, satisfaction |
| Meeting Minutes | Administrative | Attendees, time, agenda items, votes |

**Test results** across 15 meetings using the Standard Business template:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Action items correctly identified | 91% (vs. 82% for Zoom AI Companion) |
| Decision capture accuracy | 88% |
| Speaker attribution | 94% |
| Output ready to share without edits | 82% |
| Template adherence (structure match) | 96% |

The Sandler template for a sales discovery call captured budget range, pain points, and decision timeline with remarkable accuracy — output that a sales rep could paste directly into a CRM note without modification.

### Ask Fathom AI

This is Fathom's standout feature. It's a ChatGPT-like interface that searches across ALL your meetings:

- "What did the prospect say about their budget in the Q2 review call?"
- "Show me all the feature requests from customer calls this month"
- "When did the client mention they wanted to increase their contract?"

Ask Fathom responds with answers + citations — it shows you the exact meeting and timestamp where the information was mentioned. In testing:

- Single-call queries: 95% accuracy
- Cross-call queries: 85% accuracy
- Sentiment/synthesis queries (e.g., "How do customers feel about pricing?"): 78% accuracy

The cross-call search is genuinely transformative for roles that reference past conversations — sales managers preparing for quarterly reviews, product managers synthesizing user research, account managers tracking client history.

### CRM Integration & Field Sync

Fathom's CRM integration goes deeper than any competitor:

- **Native syncs**: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, and more
- **Field-level mapping**: Configure exactly which Fathom data fields map to which CRM fields
- **Automatic updates**: After every meeting, Fathom creates/updates the relevant CRM record with summary, action items, and custom fields
- **Deal View**: A dedicated page showing all meetings related to a specific deal, with synthesized insights

**Real test**: A sales rep conducting 5 discovery calls per day. With Fathom + Salesforce integration:
- Manual CRM data entry: 15-20 min/day → 0 min
- CRM note quality: Consistently structured vs. variable manual notes
- Deal View: Provides a complete meeting history without switching tools

### Coaching Metrics (Business Plan)

Fathom analyzes meeting behavior at scale:
- **Talk-to-listen ratio**: Speaker vs. listener time per participant
- **Keyword coverage**: Did the rep mention key product features, pricing, or competitors?
- **Sentiment trends**: Emotional tone throughout the call
- **Interruption frequency**: Who's interrupting whom
- **AI Scorecards**: Custom scoring rubrics for sales methodology compliance

Sales managers can review AI-generated coaching insights for their team's calls in a dashboard rather than listening to every recording. In pilot testing, teams using Fathom coaching metrics improved talk-to-listen balance by 35% over 3 months.

### Bot-Free Capture (Mac Beta)

Fathom's new bot-free mode captures meeting audio directly from your Mac without the Fathom bot joining the meeting as a participant. This means:
- No "Fathom has joined the meeting" notification
- Works for any meeting platform (Zoom, Meet, Teams)
- Feels less intrusive for external meetings

Current limitation: Mac only. Windows support is in development.

## User Experience

### Setup & Onboarding

Fathom's setup is straightforward:
1. Sign up (Google or email)
2. Install the desktop app (Mac/Windows)
3. Connect your calendar (Fathom auto-joins meetings you're invited to)
4. Configure preferred summary template (or use default)
5. Done — Fathom starts transcribing your next meeting

Setup time: 5 minutes. The meeting bot joins automatically — you don't need to manually invite it to each meeting.

### In-Meeting Experience

During meetings, Fathom is visible but non-intrusive:
- The bot appears in the participant list (unless using bot-free Mac beta)
- A sidebar shows live transcription
- The meeting host can see the AI summary being built in real-time
- Participants can add markers/comments during the call

For external/client-facing meetings, we recommend using bot-free mode (Mac) or informing participants that a meeting assistant is present. Some clients initially raised eyebrows at the "Fathom has joined" notification, though most were fine with it after explanation.

### Search & Discovery

The search experience is excellent:
- Keyword search across all meetings
- Attendee-based search
- AI search (Ask Fathom) — natural language queries
- Search alerts — auto-notify when specific topics/keywords appear in future meetings
- Smart playlists — auto-build collections of relevant clips from different meetings

## Alternatives

### Zoom AI Companion (Included with Zoom)
The "free with Zoom" option. Good for Zoom-native teams but lacks Fathom's CRM integration, summary templates, and cross-platform support. Much weaker for sales use cases. Better value for Zoom users who don't need sophisticated meeting intelligence.

### Otter.ai ($17-40/user/mo)
Stronger real-time collaboration features — shared workspaces, channels, and team notes. Otter's live transcription is slightly faster than Fathom's. However, Fathom's AI summaries are more structured and CRM integration is deeper. Better for teams that use meetings as a real-time collaboration tool; worse for sales intelligence.

### tl;dv ($18-36/user/mo)
Popular with product teams for user research. Better clip-based sharing with timestamped comments and highlight reels. Fathom is stronger for sales and customer-facing roles. Better for product research; weaker for sales coaching and CRM.

### Gong ($160/user/mo)
The enterprise revenue intelligence platform. Gong does what Fathom does but at 5x the price with much deeper analytics — deal scoring, competitive intelligence, pipeline analysis. Fathom is the "Gong for the rest of us" — 80% of the value at 15% of the price. Better for enterprise sales orgs with budgets; overkill for small teams.

### Granola ($0-19/mo)
Best for in-person meetings and the privacy-conscious user. Granola captures from device audio rather than cloud bots — useful for hybrid meetings. Much weaker for remote teams and lacks CRM integration entirely.

## FAQs

### Is Fathom AI really free?
Yes — the Free plan includes unlimited recordings, unlimited transcriptions, chronological AI summaries, clips, playlists, and search. No meeting limits, no recording duration limits. Premium features (advanced summaries, AI action items) require the Premium plan at $16/mo.

### Does Fathom work with Google Meet and Microsoft Teams?
Yes, Fathom works with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. On Mac, bot-free capture works with all three. On Windows, the Fathom bot joins the meeting. Fathom also integrates with your calendar (Google Calendar, Outlook) to automatically join scheduled meetings.

### How does Fathom handle data privacy?
Fathom is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Fathom's AI processing uses AWS and OpenAI infrastructure with data protection agreements. Business plan users get custom data retention policies. You can delete any recording at any time.

### Can Fathom replace my CRM?
No — Fathom integrates with your CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, etc.) rather than replacing it. Meeting summaries, action items, and insights are pushed into CRM records to keep them updated without manual data entry.

### What's the difference between chronological and advanced summaries?
Chronological summaries (Free) provide a timestamped timeline of the meeting — useful for reviewing what happened. Advanced summaries (Premium+) use expert frameworks like BANT or MEDDIC to structure output around specific business needs — more useful for actionable intelligence. Advanced summaries also include AI-generated action items and follow-up emails.
