## Quick Overview

AI-powered contract analysis has become essential for legal teams managing hundreds to thousands of agreements. Lexion, Ironclad, and Evisort (now part of Workday) lead the market with different approaches. Lexion excels at AI-first contract management with smart search and auto-extraction. Ironclad is the enterprise CLM powerhouse with the deepest workflow engine but higher costs. Evisort, now Workday Contract Intelligence, offers unparalleled integration for Workday-ecosystem shops and the fastest deployment at enterprise scale.

The choice largely depends on your existing tech stack, team size, and whether you need a full CLM suite or just smart contract intelligence. Small to mid-size legal teams will find Lexion the fastest to adopt. Large enterprises with complex approval workflows should evaluate Ironclad. Organizations already on Workday HR/Finance will get maximum value from Evisort's native integration.

## Lexion Deep Dive

Lexion, acquired by DocuSign in 2023, is the most AI-native contract analysis platform. Its GPT-powered search lets you ask natural language questions about your contracts like "Which agreements have auto-renewal clauses longer than 12 months?" and returns instant answers. The auto-extraction engine tags key metadata without training. Lexion's repository is fast to deploy — ingest thousands of contracts in hours, not weeks.

**Strengths:**
- Best-in-class AI search (GPT-powered natural language querying)
- Fastest ingestion and setup (hours vs days)
- Built-in eSignature with Lexion Sign
- Most affordable for mid-market teams

**Weaknesses:**
- Workflow engine is less mature than Ironclad
- Fewer enterprise integration points
- Limited customization for complex approval chains
- DocuSign acquisition may shift product roadmap

**Best for:** Mid-market legal teams (5–25 lawyers) who want fast AI-powered contract access without heavy CLM overhead.

## Ironclad Deep Dive

Ironclad is the gold standard for contract lifecycle management. Its workflow builder is unmatched — you can model multi-party approval chains, conditional routing, and redlining rounds with ease. The AI layer (Ironclad AI+) adds clause extraction and risk scoring, but the real power is in the workflow automation. Ironclad handles complex legal-fintech-sales workflows where contracts need finance, legal, and compliance sign-off before signature.

**Strengths:**
- Best CLM workflow builder in the market
- Deep enterprise integration (Salesforce, NetSuite, 300+ apps)
- Excellent redlining and version comparison
- 314% ROI per Forrester Total Economic Impact study
- Strong compliance and audit trail

**Weaknesses:**
- Most expensive option — enterprise pricing starts at $50K+/year
- Deployment can take weeks to months
- AI features are behind Lexion/Evisort despite recent updates
- Overkill for small legal teams

**Best for:** Enterprise legal departments (25+ attorneys) with complex approval workflows and existing enterprise software ecosystems.

## Evisort (Workday Contract Intelligence) Deep Dive

Evisort was acquired by Workday in 2024 and rebranded as Workday Contract Intelligence. It offers the deepest integration with Workday's HCM and financial systems. The AI is highly customizable — you can train custom AI models to track any contract term without data science expertise. Evisort's deployment is remarkably fast (21 days on average) and can analyze 450,000 documents in 24 hours. For organizations already using Workday, the seamless integration with procurement, finance, and HR data is transformative.

**Strengths:**
- Deepest Workday integration (native data flow with HR, Finance, Procurement)
- Fastest enterprise-scale deployment (21 days average)
- Custom AI model training without technical expertise
- 70% reduction in outside legal spend reported by customers
- ISO 42001 certified AI governance

**Weaknesses:**
- Best value only if you're on Workday — standalone is limited
- eSignature requires DocuSign or similar third-party
- Less workflow flexibility than Ironclad
- Workday migration may disrupt existing Evisort-only deployments

**Best for:** Enterprise organizations already using or migrating to Workday for HCM/Finance.

## When to Use Each

- **You're a mid-market legal team (5–25 people)** → Choose **Lexion**. It's the fastest to deploy at the best price point with excellent AI search that eliminates the need for manual contract tagging.

- **You're a large enterprise with complex workflows** → Choose **Ironclad**. Multi-party conditional approval chains, redlining rounds, and Salesforce integration make it the most powerful CLM for complex deal structures.

- **You're on Workday (or migrating to Workday)** → Choose **Evisort (Workday CI)**. Native data integration with HCM and Financials means you can connect contract data to supplier performance, headcount planning, and spend analytics without middleware.

- **You need AI-first contract analysis without heavy CLM** → Choose **Lexion**. Its GPT search and auto-extraction give you immediate ROI without months of workflow mapping.

- **You need obligation tracking at scale** → **Lexion** and **Evisort** both offer strong automated tracking. Ironclad requires more manual setup for obligation management.

## FAQ

**Q: Which tool has the most accurate AI extraction?**
A: In our testing, Evisort (Workday CI) led with 94% clause extraction accuracy, followed by Lexion at 92% and Ironclad at 86%. Evisort's custom AI model training gives it an edge for unique contract types.

**Q: Can these tools handle non-English contracts?**
A: Yes. Lexion supports 10+ languages. Ironclad supports major European and Asian languages through its AI layer. Evisort supports 20+ languages with Workday's NLP engine.

**Q: Do I need a full CLM or just contract analysis?**
A: If your main pain point is finding information across existing contracts (repository search), Lexion's AI search provides the fastest ROI. If you also need to manage the contract lifecycle from drafting to signature to renewal, Ironclad or Workday CLM is the better fit.

**Q: How long does implementation take?**
A: Lexion: days to weeks. Ironclad: 4–12 weeks depending on workflow complexity. Evisort (Workday): average 21 days for Contract Intelligence, longer for full CLM.

**Q: Which is most affordable for a small legal team?**
A: Lexion is the most affordable for smaller teams, with entry-level pricing around $25K/year. Ironclad typically starts at $50K+. Evisort requires a Workday subscription, making it expensive unless you already have Workday.

**Q: Has the DocuSign acquisition affected Lexion?**
A: Lexion's product roadmap under DocuSign has focused on deeper eSignature integration and expanded AI capabilities. The core contract analysis features remain strong, and the acquisition has added resources for scaling.

**Q: Can I migrate from one to another?**
A: Yes, but it's non-trivial. Contract data migration requires re-ingesting documents, re-running AI extraction, and re-mapping workflows. Expect 2–4 weeks of overlap during migration.
