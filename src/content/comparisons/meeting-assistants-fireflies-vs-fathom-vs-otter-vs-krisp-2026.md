## Introduction

The average knowledge worker spends over 15 hours per week in meetings — and forgets 40% of what was discussed within 24 hours. AI meeting assistants solve this by joining your calls, transcribing conversations, extracting action items, and producing searchable summaries. In 2026, four tools lead the pack: **Fireflies.ai**, **Fathom**, **Otter.ai**, and **Krisp**.

While they all transcribe meetings, their philosophies differ dramatically. Fireflies is an enterprise conversation search engine. Fathom focuses on actionable summaries and CRM workflows. Otter combines real-time transcription with team collaboration. Krisp starts with noise cancellation and layers meeting intelligence on top. This head-to-head comparison benchmarks them on transcription quality, AI feature depth, integrations, pricing, and overall value.

## Detailed Analysis

### Fireflies.ai — Best for Enterprise Conversation Search

Fireflies treats your meetings as a searchable knowledge base. Its killer feature is the ability to search across *all* past conversations — not just by keyword, but by concept. Ask "What did Marketing decide about the Q4 launch budget?" and Fireflies scans every relevant meeting for the answer.

The conversation intelligence features are best-in-class. Fireflies automatically detects speakers, identifies questions asked, recognizes objections and competitor mentions, and even measures talk-to-listen ratios. The Smart Summaries break meetings into chapters with timestamps and highlight key decisions.

The Salesforce integration is particularly strong — meeting notes, action items, and call outcomes sync automatically to contacts and opportunities. For sales teams tracking dozens of calls per week, this alone justifies the cost.

That said, Fireflies doesn't offer real-time transcription during the meeting itself. Transcripts appear after the call ends, and processing can take 5-15 minutes depending on meeting length. If you need live captions, look elsewhere.

**Who it's best for:** Sales teams, account managers, and growing companies that need to extract insights from hundreds of customer conversations and make them searchable across the org.

### Fathom — Best for Action-Oriented Summarization

Fathom has the most polished meeting summarization in the game. Its summaries aren't just transcripts — they're genuinely useful recaps that highlight decisions, action items, and next steps with remarkable accuracy. The "Smart Links" feature automatically links action items to relevant CRM records, Slack channels, or documents.

Fathom's UI is the cleanest of the bunch. During a meeting, you can bookmark moments with a single click, and Fathom will automatically highlight those sections in the summary. After the call, you get a structured recap with an executive summary, "Key Topics Discussed," "Decisions Made," and "Action Items" — each with timestamps linking back to the recording.

The free tier is extremely generous: unlimited recordings with AI summaries. Most individual users never need to upgrade. The paid plans unlock CRM integrations, analytics dashboards, and team features.

Fathom's weakness is that it doesn't attempt to be a conversation search engine like Fireflies. Search is limited to individual meetings, and there's no cross-meeting knowledge discovery. It also doesn't transcribe in real-time — you see the live in-meeting view, but the polished summary arrives after.

**Who it's best for:** Individual professionals, consultants, and small teams who want the best possible meeting summaries without complexity.

### Otter.ai — Best for Real-Time Collaboration

Otter is the veteran of the space, with over a decade of transcription technology refinement. Its real-time transcription accuracy is the best in the industry — roughly 95% for clear audio, and it handles accents better than competitors.

Otter is unique in serving as both a meeting bot and a collaboration workspace. After meetings, transcripts live in a shared workspace where team members can add comments, highlight passages, and create threaded discussions. The Otter AI Chat lets you query past meetings conversationally: "Summarize all mentions of the pricing redesign across my last five meetings."

OtterPilot is a standout feature — it automatically detects upcoming meetings on your calendar and joins them without requiring manual invites. It also captures and annotates shared slides, making presentations searchable.

The main drawbacks: the free tier is restrictive (300 minutes/month with a 30-minute meeting cap), and the UI can feel cluttered compared to Fathom's streamlined design. Action item extraction, while present, isn't as refined as Fathom's.

**Who it's best for:** Teams that want real-time captions during meetings and a shared space for annotating, discussing, and referencing meeting content collaboratively.

### Krisp — Best for Privacy + Noise Cancellation

Krisp takes a fundamentally different approach. Instead of a bot joining your meetings, Krisp runs locally on your device as an audio driver. It processes audio in real-time, canceling background noise on both ends while simultaneously transcribing and summarizing the conversation.

The privacy angle is compelling: no meeting data leaves your device unless you explicitly enable cloud features. For lawyers, therapists, journalists, and anyone handling confidential conversations, this is a game-changer. No bot means no awkward "who invited the AI?" moments.

Krisp's noise cancellation is legendary — it can eliminate barking dogs, construction noise, crying babies, and even echo from bad room acoustics. The meeting transcription and summary features, added in 2025, are competent but not as deep as the dedicated meeting tools.

Limitations: no CRM integrations, no team collaboration features, and the AI summaries are more basic than Fathom or Fireflies. Krisp is a noise-cancellation tool first and a meeting assistant second.

**Who it's best for:** Remote workers in noisy environments who value privacy above all else, and professionals handling confidential conversations who can't risk cloud storage.

## Which Should You Choose?

**Choose Fireflies.ai if:**
- You run 10+ customer-facing calls per week and need to search across all conversations
- Your team relies on Salesforce/HubSpot and wants meeting data to flow into CRM automatically
- You want conversation intelligence (talk ratios, objection tracking, competitor mentions)
- You have a distributed sales or customer success team

**Choose Fathom if:**
- You want the single best meeting summary experience with minimal friction
- You're an individual consultant, freelancer, or small team leader
- Action item tracking and follow-through is your top priority
- You love the free tier and want to avoid paying unless absolutely necessary

**Choose Otter.ai if:**
- Real-time live captions during meetings are non-negotiable
- You want a team collaboration space around meeting notes (comments, highlights, chat)
- You record meetings with slides and want them captured and searchable
- Transcription accuracy with diverse accents is critical

**Choose Krisp if:**
- You work from noisy environments and need reliable noise cancellation
- Privacy is paramount — you handle confidential client or patient conversations
- You find meeting bots intrusive and want invisible, local processing
- You want one tool that handles both noise cancellation and basic meeting notes

## FAQ

### Can I use more than one meeting assistant?
Yes, many users run Krisp for noise cancellation alongside Fathom or Fireflies for transcription and summarization. However, avoid having two transcription bots in the same meeting — they'll compete for audio and may cause issues.

### How do these tools handle non-English meetings?
Otter supports English only for real-time transcription, with limited support for Spanish and French in beta. Fireflies supports 60+ languages for post-meeting transcription. Fathom primarily supports English with expanding language coverage. Krisp's noise cancellation is language-agnostic, but transcription is English-only.

### Do meeting assistants work with in-person meetings?
Yes, all four can record and transcribe in-person meetings through your device's microphone. Otter has the best in-person experience with its mobile app. Krisp's noise cancellation is particularly valuable for in-person meetings in noisy offices.

### Are these tools compliant with GDPR and SOC 2?
Fireflies, Fathom, and Otter are SOC 2 Type II compliant and offer GDPR-compliant data processing. Krisp processes everything locally by default, making it the most privacy-respecting option. Enterprise plans from all providers include data residency options.

### Do meeting participants know the AI is recording?
Transparency varies. Fireflies, Fathom, and Otter join as visible bot participants — meeting hosts see them in the participant list. Krisp is invisible (local audio processing). Most organizations require explicit consent before recording, and some meeting platforms can auto-notify participants. Always check your local laws and company policies.
