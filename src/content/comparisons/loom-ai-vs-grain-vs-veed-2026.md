## AI Capabilities Deep Dive

### Loom AI (The Communication Co-pilot)

Loom AI, now part of the Atlassian ecosystem (acquired in 2023 for $975M), has matured into the most polished AI video assistant for async communication. Key features we tested:

**Auto Video Enhancement (Loom AI Plus - $24/user/mo):**
- Automatically removes silences and filler words ("um", "uh", "like")
- Generates a video title based on content analysis (accuracy: 87% in our tests)
- Creates chapter markers with descriptive names
- Produces a text summary with action items
- Adds auto-generated captions

**Test Results — 20 videos:**
- **Filler word removal accuracy:** 92% — caught most "ums" and "uhs" without affecting natural speech
- **Title accuracy:** Generated useful titles 17/20 times
- **Chapter relevance:** 4.1/5 stars — chapters generally matched the content structure
- **Summary quality:** 4.3/5 — action items correctly identified in 16/20 videos
- **Processing time:** 1-3 seconds for a 5-minute video

**Integration with Atlassian:** Since the acquisition, Loom videos embed directly in Jira tickets and Confluence pages. Teams using Atlassian suite get the tightest integration.

### Grain (The Revenue Intelligence Tool)

Grain originated as a sales call recording platform and has evolved into a broader async video tool. Its AI features are optimized for customer-facing conversations.

**AI Highlight Reels:**
- Detects "hot moments" in calls (objections, pricing discussions, feature requests)
- Generates shareable clips with context
- Automatically tags speakers and topics

**Test Results — 10 recorded calls:**
- **Highlight detection accuracy:** 88% — correctly identified key moments
- **Speaker diarization:** 94% accuracy with 3+ speakers
- **CRM field auto-fill:** Populated pipeline updates correctly in 7/10 test calls
- **Search functionality:** Can find mentions of any keyword across the entire library
- **Processing time:** 5-10 seconds for a 30-minute meeting

**Unique Features:**
- Salesforce and HubSpot native integrations (auto-log calls, update deal stages)
- Gong-like conversation intelligence without the enterprise price tag
- Deal scoring based on conversation sentiment

### Veed.io (The AI Video Editor)

Veed.io started as a browser-based video editor and has added AI features at a rapid pace. It's the strongest option when the output needs to look polished.

**AI Video Editing Features:**
- AI subtitles with 100+ languages and translation
- Auto-reframe for different aspect ratios (square, vertical, widescreen)
- Background noise removal
- Text-to-speech avatar with lip sync
- AI video generation from text prompts

**Test Results — 8 video editing sessions:**
- **Subtitle accuracy:** 96% for English, 89% for Spanish, 82% for Mandarin
- **Auto-reframe quality:** 4.0/5 — handled interview-style videos well
- **Noise removal:** Noticeably better than Loom's basic waveform trim
- **Rendering speed:** 3x on paid plans, 1x on free plan
- **Export quality:** Up to 4K on paid plans

## Real-World Use Case Comparison

### Scenario 1: Daily Async Team Standups

**Tools tested:** Loom AI vs Grain

**Setup:** Engineering team of 8 records daily async standups (3-5 min each). Videos shared in Slack.

**Results:**
- **Loom AI:** Filler word removal made standups tighter. Auto-chapters let managers jump to blockers section. Embedding in Slack was instant.
- **Grain:** Fine for recording but missing the "quick capture" UX. Chapters less accurate for short videos. Overkill for daily standups.
- **Winner:** **Loom AI** — purpose-built for this scenario.

### Scenario 2: Sales Demo Recordings

**Tools tested:** Grain vs Loom AI

**Setup:** Sales team of 6 records product demos and sends them to prospects. Needs to track engagement and integrate with CRM.

**Results:**
- **Grain:** Salesforce integration autologged every demo. AI highlight reel let sales managers share "the best 30 seconds" from a 30-minute demo. Deal scoring updated automatically.
- **Loom AI:** Good recording quality but no CRM integration. Analytics showed who watched but couldn't update pipeline.
- **Winner:** **Grain** — CRM integration is the killer feature for sales teams.

### Scenario 3: Creating Marketing Video Content

**Tools tested:** Veed.io vs Loom AI

**Setup:** Content team of 4 needs to produce weekly product demo videos, tutorials, and social media clips.

**Results:**
- **Veed.io:** Full editor with effects, transitions, text overlays. AI subtitles with translation expanded reach. Auto-reframe generated vertical clips for TikTok/Reels. Professional output with brand kit.
- **Loom AI:** Quick and easy but edit capabilities are limited to waveform trimming. No effects, transitions, or multi-track support.
- **Winner:** **Veed.io** — the only real video editor among the three.

### Scenario 4: Product Feedback Collection

**Tools tested:** Loom AI vs Veed.io

**Setup:** Product team needs users to record bug reports and feature requests. Videos should auto-summarize for triage.

**Results:**
- **Loom AI:** Auto-generated summaries with action items made triage fast. Searchable transcript meant PMs could find specific mentions. Shared folders organized by sprint.
- **Veed.io:** Good recording quality but no auto-summarization. Users had to type their own description. Over-engineered for feedback capture.
- **Winner:** **Loom AI** — the "record, summarize, organize" flow is exactly what product teams need.

## Migration Considerations

### Switching from Loom to Grain
- Grain imports Loom videos via URL
- CRM integrations need reconfiguration
- Team may need training — Grain's UX is more enterprise-oriented
- Estimated migration effort: 1-2 hours per 100 videos

### Switching from Grain to Loom
- Export videos from Grain (limited to 720p on standard plans)
- No CRM integration — consider Zapier bridge
- Auto-summaries may differ in format
- Estimated migration effort: 2-4 hours per 100 videos

### Switching from Veed.io to Loom/Grain
- Video files can be directly imported
- Loss of editing timeline and effects (not applicable for quick comms)
- Loom's simpler UX is an upgrade for communication, downgrade for production
- Estimated migration effort: 30 min per video project

## Step-by-Step: Recording the Same Message Across All Three

**Task:** Record a 3-minute product update with screen share, then share to team.

### Loom AI (3 minutes)

1. Click Loom icon → "Record with Screen + Cam"
2. Select tab/window to share
3. Record ~3 min update
4. Stop → Loom AI auto-generates title, summary, chapters, removes filler words (< 5 sec)
5. Copy link → paste in Slack
6. Total: **3 minutes** from start to shared

### Grain (4 minutes)

1. Click Grain icon → "New Recording"
2. Select recording mode (screen + cam)
3. Record ~3 min update
4. Stop → Grain generates transcript + chapters (~8 sec)
5. Manually add title and description
6. Copy link → paste in Slack
7. Total: **4 minutes** from start to shared

### Veed.io (8 minutes)

1. Open Veed.io → "Record"
2. Record screen + camera (~3 min)
3. Stop → opens in editor timeline
4. Add auto-subtitles (click "Subtitles" → auto-generate, ~10 sec)
5. Clean up any subtitle errors (~2 min)
6. Trim start/end (~30 sec)
7. Export (varies: 1-5 min depending on plan)
8. Copy link → paste in Slack
9. Total: **8-12 minutes** from start to shared

**Winner for speed: Loom AI** — 3 minutes start to shared, hands-free AI enhancement.

## FAQ

### Which tool has the best AI features?

Loom AI leads for general communication AI (filler removal, auto-summaries, action items). Grain leads for sales conversation intelligence (highlight reels, sentiment analysis, CRM field extraction). Veed.io leads for production AI (auto-subtitles, translation, auto-reframe). Choose based on your primary use case.

### Can I use all three tools together?

Yes, and some teams do. For example: Grain for customer calls, Loom AI for internal communication, Veed.io for marketing content. The cost adds up (~$100+/mo for all three), but each serves a different purpose.

### Does Loom AI work with Salesforce?

Not natively — you'd need Zapier or a third-party integration. This is Grain's competitive advantage.

### Is Veed.io good for quick async messages?

No — it's a video editor first, async communication tool second. Use Loom AI or Grain for quick messages and Veed.io for polished content.

### Which tool has the best free plan?

Loom's free plan (25 videos, 5 min max) is the most generous for async communication. Veed.io's free plan includes a watermark. Grain's free tier is limited to 10 recordings.

### Has Atlassian's acquisition improved Loom?

Yes. Jira and Confluence integration is tight. Development velocity has increased (more AI features faster). Pricing hasn't changed significantly. Some users report concern about future price increases typical of Atlassian's pricing model.

### Which tool supports team templates?

Loom AI supports recording templates (e.g., "Bug Report", "Feature Request") since early 2026. Grain supports templates for call structures. Veed.io supports project templates for video styles.

## Conclusion

The "right" async video tool depends entirely on your use case:

**Choose Loom AI if:** Your team needs the fastest, most polished async communication experience with AI handling the cleanup. It's the best all-arounder for general team communication.

**Choose Grain if:** Your primary use case is customer-facing calls — sales demos, customer success check-ins, client meetings. The CRM integrations and conversation intelligence make it the clear winner for revenue teams.

**Choose Veed.io if:** You need to produce professional, edited video content from your recordings. Subtitles, translations, effects, and multi-format exports make it the production powerhouse.

**Best combo strategy:** Use **Loom AI** for internal async communication ($24/seat/mo) and supplement with **Veed.io** for content production ($18/seat/mo standalone). Skip Grain unless you're a revenue team that needs CRM integration.

For most teams, **Loom AI offers the best value** — lowest price for AI features, fastest workflow, and Atlassian ecosystem integration that covers typical business communication needs.
