# AI Note-Takers 2026: Granola vs Tactiq vs Otter — Deep Comparison

AI meeting notetakers have become essential tools for remote and hybrid teams in 2026. Three apps lead the category: Granola, Tactiq, and Otter.ai. Each approaches the problem differently — Granola is a local-first AI scribe for Mac, Tactiq is a lightweight Chrome extension, and Otter is a full-featured transcription and collaboration platform.

Granola produces the most useful meeting summaries by combining local audio processing with LLM-powered structuring. Tactiq is the fastest to deploy and best for Google Meet users. Otter is the most feature-rich but the slowest and most expensive.

## Transcription Accuracy

Granola runs locally on your Mac, achieving 97-99% accuracy on English meetings without internet dependency. It doesn't use a cloud transcription service — the processing happens on-device with periodic LLM calls for summary generation. Tactiq uses Google's speech-to-text via the Chrome API, achieving 93-95% accuracy. Otter's proprietary transcription engine hits 94-96% but struggles with accents and technical jargon.

Granola's local processing is its key advantage. No audio leaves your machine, and transcripts are available even without an internet connection. Tactiq and Otter both require cloud connectivity.

## Meeting Summary Quality

Granola generates structured notes with action items, decisions, key discussion points, and a "before/after" summary that captures context from previous meetings. Its template system lets you customize the output for different meeting types — one-on-ones, standups, client calls.

Tactiq's summaries are shorter and more formulaic. They work well for quick reference but miss nuance. Otter produces comprehensive transcripts with AI-generated summaries, but the summaries tend to be verbose — you often end up reading the whole meeting anyway.

## Integrations

Tactiq integrates with Google Meet, Zoom, and Teams via browser extension — no installation needed. Granola acts as a system audio capture app that works with any meeting app (Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack Huddles, even FaceTime). Otter connects natively with Zoom, Google Calendar, and Teams but requires Otter's own meeting bot to join calls.

Granola's universal capture is a standout feature. It works with any app that outputs audio, without needing a bot to join your calendar events.

## Action Item Extraction

Granola reliably extracts and assigns action items in structured format. You can push action items directly to Linear, Notion, Asana, or Todoist. Otter handles action items but the extraction quality varies — about 70% of identified action items are genuinely actionable. Tactiq identifies action items only on the Pro plan, and the extraction is less consistent than Granola's.

## Use Case Recommendations

**For independent professionals and Mac users**: Granola Pro ($20/mo). The local processing, high accuracy, and universal capture justify the cost. One user on G2 noted: "Granola's 'before and after' view is the killer feature — it knows what I discussed with a client last week without me searching."

**For Google Meet power users on a budget**: Tactiq Pro ($12/mo). Fast setup, lightweight, and good enough for daily standups and internal meetings.

**For teams that need full transcription archives**: Otter Business ($30/user/mo). The searchable transcript library and speaker identification are valuable for compliance and knowledge management.

**For privacy-conscious users**: Granola. No cloud audio processing means no third party hears your meetings. This is critical for legal, medical, and financial conversations.

## Limitations

Granola is Mac-only with no web or Windows version planned as of mid-2026. Tactiq lacks offline functionality and struggles with hour-long meetings (browser memory issues). Otter's bot-based joining approach feels intrusive — team members report feeling surveilled.

## The Bottom Line

Granola wins for independent professionals and Mac users who value privacy and summary quality. Tactiq is the best entry-level choice. Otter remains the best option for enterprise compliance and search use cases. Try Granola and Tactiq for free first — Otter's bot-join model is a dealbreaker for many teams.
