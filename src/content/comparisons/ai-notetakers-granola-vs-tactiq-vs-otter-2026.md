# Granola vs Tactiq vs Otter — AI Note Taker Deep Comparison (2026)

Meetings are the silent productivity killer of the modern workplace. By 2026, AI note takers have become as essential as calendar apps — but they've diverged into three distinct approaches. Granola runs invisibly on your computer, Tactiq lives in your browser as a Chrome extension, and Otter.ai joins meetings as a visible bot.

We tested all three extensively to understand which approach delivers the best results.

## Quick Verdict

**Granola** wins on social intelligence — its "no bot" approach means meetings feel natural, and its AI note quality is excellent. **Tactiq** wins on frictionless installation and developer-friendly features like MCP connectors. **Otter** is the most mature platform with the deepest integration ecosystem, ideal for enterprises that need Salesforce sync and compliance.

Each serves a fundamentally different user profile, making this comparison more about philosophy than features.

## Granola: The Discreet Notepad

Granola's core innovation is simple but profound: **it doesn't join your meeting.** Instead, Granola runs as a desktop app on macOS or Windows, capturing your computer's audio while you participate normally. No bot announces itself, no participant list shows a mysterious attendee, and no one feels like they're being recorded.

### How It Works

Granola syncs with your calendar and automatically detects upcoming meetings. When a meeting starts, Granola begins transcribing in the background — it uses your computer's audio output and microphone, so it captures everything you hear and say. After the meeting ends, Granola generates notes, action items, and a summary within seconds.

### Key Features

**Meeting Prep Briefs**: Before every meeting, Granola creates a brief showing who's attending, what you discussed last time, and what's relevant now. It pulls context from calendar events, past notes, and email patterns.

**Chat Based on Meeting Memory**: Granola's chat feature has a remarkable ability — it already knows what you discussed. You can ask "What did I promise to do this week?" and get a comprehensive list pulled from all your meetings.

**MCP Connector**: For developers, Granola's MCP (Model Context Protocol) connector lets AI assistants read and query meeting notes seamlessly. This integration with tools like Claude Code makes meeting intelligence accessible from any automation workflow.

### Pricing

Granola takes a generous approach to its free tier:
- **Free**: Unlimited meeting notes, stored for 30 days
- **Pro**: View and work with notes older than 30 days (pricing not publicly listed — contact sales)

The free tier is unusually generous compared to competitors. Granola recently raised $125M in Series C funding, signaling strong investor confidence in this approach.

### Who It's For

Granola is ideal for knowledge workers who attend 5+ meetings daily and want meeting intelligence without changing meeting dynamics. It's particularly well-suited for managers, product leaders, and consultants who value interpersonal dynamics and don't want a bot altering team behavior.

> "Granola was one of those things that just clicked instantly. It's seamless and very purpose-built." — Karri Saarinen, CEO of Linear

## Tactiq: The Chrome Extension Powerhouse

Tactiq takes the opposite approach — it's a Chrome extension that adds AI transcription and note-taking to your existing meeting flows. No installation required beyond the browser, making it the fastest tool to start using.

### How It Works

Install the Chrome extension, and Tactiq automatically detects when you're in a Google Meet, Zoom (web), or Teams (web) meeting. It provides real-time transcription and AI-generated insights during and after the meeting. The key differentiator: **Tactiq doesn't need to join the meeting as a participant** — it works from within your browser tab.

### Key Features

**AI Workflow Builder**: Create custom AI workflows that run automatically after every meeting — generate summaries, extract action items, draft follow-up emails, and push to Notion or Google Drive.

**In-Meeting AI**: During the meeting, Tactiq provides real-time suggestions, notes, and searchable transcripts without leaving the meeting window.

**Unlimited AI Credits**: On Team and Business plans, AI credits are unlimited — a significant advantage for heavy meeting users who want to run multiple AI queries per meeting.

**Developer-Friendly Stack**: Tactiq offers MCP (beta) and Claude Connector (beta) integrations, making meeting data accessible to coding assistants and automation pipelines. This is unique among the three — no other notetaker has this level of developer tool integration.

### Who It's For

Tactiq is perfect for individuals and teams who want the fastest possible setup and don't want to install yet another desktop app. Its Chrome-extension-first approach means it's ideal for sales teams, customer support, and anyone who lives in Google Workspace.

## Otter.ai: The Enterprise Standard

Otter.ai is the most established of the three, having been in the AI transcription space since 2016. Its approach is to join meetings as a visible bot that transcribes, summarizes, and provides AI-powered insights.

### How It Works

Otter.ai integrates with your calendar and automatically joins your scheduled meetings as a virtual participant. Meeting participants see "Otter.ai" in the participant list and can interact with it — users can highlight text, add comments, and assign action items in real-time.

### Key Features

**AI Meeting Templates**: Pro plans include advanced meeting templates that customize how Otter generates summaries, action items, and outlines based on meeting type (sales call, standup, 1:1, etc.).

**Multi-Language Support**: Otter transcribes in English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Chinese — the broadest language support of the three.

**CRM Integrations**: Business plan includes Salesforce and HubSpot integrations, syncing meeting notes and action items directly to CRM records. This is a game-changer for sales teams.

**Otter MCP Server**: For developers who want to connect Otter data to their AI tools.

### Pricing

### Who It's For

Otter is best for organizations that need enterprise-grade features — especially sales teams that need CRM syncing, compliance-aware industries that require HIPAA, and large organizations with SSO requirements.

## Granola's Secret Weapon: Meeting Prep Briefs

One feature sets Granola apart from both competitors: **automatic meeting preparation briefs.** Before every meeting, Granola analyzes your calendar, past notes, and contact information to generate a brief: who's attending, what you discussed last time, what's changed, and what matters now.

This transforms Granola from a passive note-taker into an active meeting preparation tool — a significant productivity multiplier for anyone in recurring meetings.

Otter and Tactiq are reactive (they capture what happened during the meeting). Granola is also proactive (it prepares you for what's coming).

## FAQ

**Q: Does Granola really not join the meeting?**
A: Yes. Granola runs as a desktop app on your computer, capturing your computer's audio. No bot appears in the meeting participant list, and no one else knows Granola is capturing the conversation. This is both its biggest strength (natural meetings) and a consideration (some organizations require consent disclosure).

**Q: Which platform has the best free tier?**
A: Granola offers the most generous free tier — unlimited meeting notes with 30-day history. Tactiq's free tier gives 10 transcripts per month, while Otter provides 300 transcription minutes monthly. For heavy meeting users, Granola's free tier is far more usable.

**Q: Can I use these tools for in-person meetings?**
A: Granola has iOS and Android apps specifically for in-person capture. Otter also has mobile apps. Tactiq is primarily for virtual meetings through its Chrome extension.

**Q: Are my meeting transcripts private?**
A: Granola processes locally on your device and is private by default. Tactiq and Otter use cloud processing but maintain SOC 2, GDPR, and ISO 27001 certifications. Otter offers HIPAA compliance as an add-on.
