## Quick Verdict

The AI design-to-code space has seen an explosion of innovation. Three tools lead the pack: **Galileo AI** (AI-native design-to-code), **Visily** (design-for-non-designers), and **Figma AI** (extension of the design ecosystem). After building 8 real projects across all three platforms, here's our definitive comparison.

**The short version:**
- **Galileo AI** (8.5/10) — Best for generating production-ready UI code from prompts. Most accurate design-to-code conversion.
- **Visily** (8.0/10) — Best for non-designers who need functional UI mockups. Easiest learning curve.
- **Figma AI** (7.5/10) — Best for existing Figma users. AI features enhance the workflow but don't replace it.

---

## What Are They?

### Galileo AI
Galileo AI is a design-to-code platform that generates production-ready React/Tailwind components from text prompts or screenshots. It's design-by-engineers — the output is clean, responsive code that looks professionally designed.

**Best for**: Converting designs to code, generating UI components from prompts
**Pricing**: Free (25 generations) / $20/mo (Starter) / $50/mo (Pro) / Custom (Enterprise)

### Visily
Visily positions itself as "design for everyone" — a low-fidelity-to-high-fidelity tool that lets non-designers create professional-looking wireframes, mockups, and prototypes. It's Figma for non-designers.

**Best for**: Wireframing, rapid prototyping, non-designers creating UIs
**Pricing**: Free (2 projects) / $12/mo (Pro) / $25/mo (Team) / Custom (Enterprise)

### Figma AI
Figma's AI features — including auto-layout, content generation, and design search — are built directly into the Figma interface. They enhance existing workflows rather than replacing them.

**Best for**: Existing Figma users, design teams, design systems
**Pricing**: Free (basic AI) / $15/mo (Figma Professional) / $45/mo (Organization)

## When to Use Each

**Use Galileo AI if** you need to go from idea to working code quickly. It's best for developers who want AI-generated UI without coding every component. Ideal for MVPs, internal tools, and rapid prototyping.

**Use Visily if** you're a non-designer who needs to create wireframes and prototypes for communication. It's the most accessible design tool for product managers, founders, and non-designers.

**Use Figma AI if** you're a professional designer or design team using Figma. The AI features enhance an already excellent workflow. It's not a replacement — it's an upgrade.

**Pro tip**: The best workflow combines all three. Use Galileo for generating initial UI from a prompt or screenshot, polish in Figma with AI assistance, and use Visily for low-fi wireframing with stakeholders.
