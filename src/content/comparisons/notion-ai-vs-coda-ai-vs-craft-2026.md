## AI Feature Deep Dive

### Notion AI Core

Notion's AI is the most deeply integrated into the platform. It exists in every part of the experience:

- **AI Sidebar:** Ask questions about your entire workspace. "What's the status of Project Orion?" gets an answer synthesized from all relevant pages, databases, and comments.
- **Autofill:** AI populates database fields based on existing data patterns. Particularly useful for CRM-like databases where you want AI to enrich contact records.
- **Notion Agent (Beta):** An autonomous agent that can perform research, summarize documents, and complete tasks based on your instructions. It operates in the background and returns results to your workspace.
- **Custom Agents (Credit-based):** Build AI agents that handle repetitive tasks autonomously. Configure them with specific instructions, tools, and data sources. Costs $10 per 1,000 Notion credits.
- **AI Meeting Notes:** Automatically generates meeting summaries from connected calendars.
- **Enterprise Search:** AI-powered search across your entire workspace with natural language queries.

### Coda AI

Coda takes a different approach — AI is integrated into the document structure rather than a separate sidebar experience:

- **AI Packs:** AI functionality lives in "Packs" — extensions that add capabilities to your docs. You can build custom AI workflows by connecting AI Packs to other Packs (Google Sheets, Slack, Jira).
- **AI Formula Assistant:** Generate table formulas in natural language — "show me all tasks due this week assigned to me, sorted by priority" becomes a working formula.
- **AI Summary & Rewrite:** Inline AI for summarizing sections, rewriting content, and generating tables from text.
- **Doc-wide AI Search:** Natural language queries search across all your Coda docs.

### Craft AI

Craft's AI is designed for writers and individual creators:

- **AI Assistant:** Available via sidebar or keyboard shortcut. Generates content, rewrites text, fixes grammar, adjusts tone.
- **Core, Fast, and Max Models:** Three AI model tiers — Core for quick tasks, Fast for daily writing, Max for complex creative work.
- **AI Prompt Library:** Curated writing prompts for common tasks (blog posts, meeting notes, project briefs).
- **Limitation:** 15 AI credits/month on Free, 50/month on Plus. Heavy AI users may hit limits.
- **Focus:** AI enhances the writing experience rather than automating workflows.

## Pros and Cons Per Tool

### Notion AI

**Pros:**
- Most comprehensive AI workspace — agents, search, meeting notes, autofill
- Custom Agents enable autonomous task automation
- Unmatched database capabilities (subtaks, dependencies, formulas, charts)
- Massive template library (10,000+)
- Strong enterprise security (SAML SSO, SCIM, audit log, zero data retention option)
- Notion Mail and Calendar integration creates a unified productivity hub

**Cons:**
- AI add-on is $10/mo per member on top of base subscription
- Notion Agent and Custom Agents are credit-based — heavy usage costs extra
- Performance can lag with large workspaces
- Document formatting less polished than Craft
- Learning curve is significant for new users

### Coda AI

**Pros:**
- Best at turning documents into interactive apps with buttons, tables, and automations
- AI Packs integrate with 100+ external tools for customized workflows
- Natural language formula builder is excellent for non-technical users
- Cleaner document structure than Notion for data-heavy use cases
- Real-time collaboration is smooth and responsive

**Cons:**
- No autonomous AI agents — limited to inline assistance
- Fewer templates than Notion (500+ vs 10,000+)
- AI features feel bolted on rather than deeply integrated
- Smaller community and ecosystem than Notion
- Table functionality is powerful but has a learning curve

### Craft

**Pros:**
- Most beautiful, distraction-free writing environment
- AI assistant that genuinely improves writing quality
- No separate AI subscription — AI included in plan pricing
- Excellent offline experience on Mac
- Clean, intuitive interface — almost no learning curve
- API and MCP access for developers

**Cons:**
- No databases or structured data — basic lists only
- No AI agents or automation capabilities
- AI credit system (50/mo) limits heavy AI usage
- Limited enterprise features (no SSO, no audit logs)
- Smallest template library and integration ecosystem
- Primarily macOS/iOS focused

## Use Case Recommendations

### For Teams Building a Company Wiki + Project Hub

**Winner: Notion AI.** Notion's database-driven pages, AI-powered search, and Custom Agents make it the best platform for building an internal knowledge base. The Notion Agent can automatically update project status, research competitors, and maintain documentation.

**Stack:** Notion Business ($18/seat/mo) + AI add-on ($10/seat/mo)

### For Structured Documents That Behave Like Apps

**Winner: Coda AI.** If your documents need buttons, tables, automations, and integrations — Coda turns docs into tools. Example: a hiring doc that automatically posts to Slack, tracks candidates in a table, and sends email via a Pack.

**Stack:** Coda Team ($30/seat/mo) + AI add-on ($10/seat/mo)

### For Writers and Individual Creators

**Winner: Craft.** If beautiful writing is your primary use case, Craft's distraction-free interface combined with its AI writing assistant creates the best authoring experience. No databases, no project management — just clean, focused document creation.

**Stack:** Craft Plus (¥699/year)

### For Enterprise Knowledge Management

**Winner: Notion AI.** SSO, SCIM, audit logs, zero data retention options, and Enterprise Search make Notion the enterprise-ready choice. Custom Agents add autonomous capabilities that no competitor matches.

**Stack:** Notion Enterprise (custom) + AI add-on

### For Budget-Conscious AI Users

**Winner: Craft.** Craft's Plus plan includes AI at no extra cost (50 credits/month). For a single user, that's ~$10/month total vs $20/month for Notion AI or Coda AI (base + AI add-on).

## FAQ

### Can I use AI to search across my entire workspace?

Notion offers the deepest workspace-wide AI search with its Enterprise Search feature. Coda supports doc-wide AI search. Craft's AI works per-document — no workspace-wide search yet.

### Which platform has the best AI writing assistant?

Craft's AI produces the highest quality writing output for creative tasks. Notion AI is better for functional writing (meeting notes, project updates). Coda AI is best for data-driven writing (reports with table analysis).

### Do any of these platforms have AI agents?

Only Notion offers Custom Agents and Notion Agent — autonomous AI that performs research and tasks in the background. Coda and Craft do not have AI agent capabilities.

### Can I use these tools offline?

Craft has the best offline experience as a native Mac/iOS app. Notion supports offline for selected pages. Coda works offline with limitations.

### Which platform is easiest to learn?

Craft has the gentlest learning curve — it's a beautiful text editor with AI. Notion has the steepest due to its database system. Coda is somewhere in between.

### Do all platforms support real-time collaboration?

Yes. All three support real-time collaborative editing. Notion and Coda are stronger for team collaboration, while Craft is more individual-focused (though Shared Spaces enable collaboration).

## The Verdict

In the 2026 AI workspace landscape, the choice comes down to your core workflow:

- **Notion AI** is the Swiss Army knife — it does everything, and does most things well. If you want one platform that handles wikis, project management, databases, AI agents, and collaboration under one roof, this is your answer.

- **Coda AI** is the craftsman's toolkit — best for turning documents into structured, interactive tools that integrate with your tech stack. If your work is data-heavy and process-driven, Coda's doc-as-app model is unmatched.

- **Craft** is the writer's sanctuary — best for individuals and small teams who prioritize beautiful, focused writing with AI assistance that doesn't get in the way.

Consider your primary use case: full workspace → Notion. Structured documents → Coda. Beautiful writing → Craft. For most teams, Notion AI offers the best overall value, especially with the new Custom Agents feature.
