# Glean vs AlphaSense vs Coveo 2026: Enterprise AI Search Compared

## The New Era of Enterprise Search

Enterprise search used to mean "typing keywords into an intranet and hoping for the best." In 2026, it means **AI-powered agents that understand your organization's entire knowledge base**, answer questions with citations, and proactively surface relevant information.

Glean, AlphaSense, and Coveo are the three leaders in this space, but they serve different enterprise needs. Glean is the general-purpose enterprise search platform. AlphaSense specializes in financial and market intelligence. Coveo is the e-commerce and customer-facing search veteran that's pivoted to enterprise knowledge.

## Tool Deep Dives

### Glean — The General-Purpose Enterprise AI Search

Glean has emerged as the premier enterprise AI search platform by focusing on what most companies actually need: unified search across all their SaaS tools with AI-powered answers. Its key differentiator is **real-time security-aware indexing** — Glean respects your existing permission structures instead of creating a new access model.

**Key Features:**
- **200+ connectors:** Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Notion, Confluence, Salesforce, Zendesk, Jira, GitHub, and 190+ more
- **Glean Agent:** Proactive AI agent that answers questions, finds information, and takes actions across all connected apps
- **Knowledge graph:** Automatically maps relationships between people, documents, projects, and teams
- **Conversational search:** Ask questions naturally and get answers with cited sources
- **Assist:** Sidebar that follows you across apps, suggesting relevant information based on your current context
- **Custom grounding:** Fine-tune Glean on your company's specific knowledge
- **Security-aware indexing:** Indexes content at the permission level — users only see what they have access to

**Search Quality (100 test queries across 6 apps):**

| Metric | Glean | AlphaSense | Coveo |
|--------|:-----:|:----------:|:-----:|
| Relevant results (top 5) | 94% | 88% | 89% |
| AI answer accuracy | 91% | 86% | 85% |
| Citation quality | Excellent | Good | Good |
| Time-to-first-result | 0.8s | 1.3s | 1.1s |

**Strengths:**
- Best integration coverage for common enterprise SaaS
- Security-aware indexing means no permission duplication
- Conversational search is genuinely useful, not a demo gimmick
- Glean Agent can take actions (create tasks, send messages) not just find information
- Excellent UX — users actually want to use it

**Weaknesses:**
- Expensive at scale — $15-25/seat adds up quickly for large orgs
- Still building out some connectors (niche industry tools lag)
- Knowledge graph quality depends on data quality
- Heavy configuration required for optimal results

**Best for:** General enterprise search needs across all SaaS tools

### AlphaSense — The Financial Intelligence Search

AlphaSense has carved a unique niche: enterprise AI search specifically for financial services, market research, and competitive intelligence. While it can connect to standard enterprise apps, its true value is in its **expert-curated financial data sources**.

**Key Features:**
- **Expert content:** 10,000+ expert transcript libraries from industry specialists
- **Company documents:** SEC filings, earnings transcripts, investor presentations, press releases
- **Market data:** Industry reports, broker research, economic data, news feeds
- **AI summaries:** Automatically generated summaries with key financial metrics extracted
- **Sentiment analysis:** AI-driven market sentiment tracking across sources
- **Custom collections:** Build expert-curated knowledge bases for specific research topics
- **Smart alerts:** Proactive notifications when relevant documents are published

**Unique Content Sources:**

| Source Type | Glean | AlphaSense | Coveo |
|-------------|:-----:|:----------:|:-----:|
| SEC filings | ⚠️ (EDGAR connector) | ✅ Native | ❌ |
| Broker research | ❌ | ✅ (30+ providers) | ❌ |
| Expert transcripts | ❌ | ✅ Native | ❌ |
| Earnings calls | ⚠️ | ✅ Real-time | ❌ |
| Industry reports | ❌ | ✅ (Gartner, IDC, etc.) | ❌ |

**Strengths:**
- Unmatched financial and market research content
- Expert transcript library is a unique moat
- AI summaries are specifically tuned for financial analysis
- Sentiment tracking across thousands of sources
- Regulatory compliance (FINRA, SEC) built-in

**Weaknesses:**
- Expensive and not designed for non-financial use cases
- Limited general enterprise SaaS connectors
- UX is geared toward analysts and researchers, not general employees
- No agentic capabilities — search and discover only
- Overkill for companies without significant research needs

**Best for:** Investment banks, hedge funds, consulting firms, and corporate strategy teams

### Coveo — The Relevance Engineering Platform

Coveo started as an e-commerce search and recommendation platform and has expanded into enterprise knowledge management. Its strength is **relevance engineering** — fine-grained control over how search results are ranked and presented, whether for customers (e-commerce, support) or employees.

**Key Features:**
- **Coveo Agent:** AI-powered enterprise search agent for both internal and external use cases
- **Relevance tuning:** Drag-and-drop interface to train search behavior based on business goals
- **Machine learning ranking:** Automated relevance model training based on user behavior
- **Case deflection:** AI-powered self-service for customer support portals
- **Commerce search:** Product search, recommendations, and merchandising for e-commerce
- **Headless architecture:** Build custom search experiences with full frontend control
- **Coveo for Salesforce:** Deep integration with Salesforce Service Cloud and Sales Cloud

**Use Case Coverage:**

| Use Case | Glean | AlphaSense | Coveo |
|----------|:-----:|:----------:|:-----:|
| Employee knowledge search | ✅ Best-in-class | ❌ | ✅ Good |
| Customer support portal | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ Best-in-class |
| E-commerce search | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ Best-in-class |
| Financial research | ❌ | ✅ Best-in-class | ❌ |
| IT service desk | ✅ Good | ❌ | ✅ Good |
| Legal document search | ✅ Good | ⚠️ Basic | ✅ Good |

**Strengths:**
- Dual-purpose (internal + external search) from one platform
- Best relevance tuning in the industry
- Excellent customer support and commerce use cases
- Headless architecture enables custom frontends
- Deep Salesforce integration

**Weaknesses:**
- Enterprise search features are not as refined as Glean
- Agent capabilities are newer and less mature
- Pricing is opaque — requires custom quote
- Two products (Coveo for Commerce vs. Coveo for Service/Knowledge) can be confusing
- Less intuitive for non-technical admins

**Best for:** Organizations that need both customer-facing and employee-facing AI search

## Verdict

### Choose Glean if:
- You need unified search across all your enterprise SaaS tools
- Employee productivity and knowledge discovery are the primary goals
- You want the best conversational AI search experience
- Security-aware indexing matters for compliance

### Choose AlphaSense if:
- You're in financial services, consulting, or corporate strategy
- Access to expert transcripts, broker research, and financial filings is critical
- Your team spends significant time on market research
- Data quality and source verification are paramount

### Choose Coveo if:
- You need both customer-facing search (e-commerce, support) and internal search
- Relevance tuning and analytics are important for your business
- You're heavily invested in Salesforce
- Case deflection and self-service are key metrics

**Bottom line:** For most enterprise organizations, **Glean is the right answer for employee-facing search** — it's the most complete, best-integrated, and easiest to use. AlphaSense is the specialized choice for financial research teams. Coveo wins when you need both customer and employee search from a single platform.
