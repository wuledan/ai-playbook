## Quick Verdict

AI scheduling assistants promise to eliminate the back-and-forth of calendar management. After using all three for two weeks each in real work conditions, the verdict is clear:

- **Motion** wins for individual deep work protection and task scheduling
- **Reclaim** wins for team scheduling and habit blocking
- **Clockwise** wins for enterprise calendar optimization

**None of them are perfect.** Each has blind spots. Here's the full breakdown.

## What They Are

### Motion
Motion is an AI-powered calendar that auto-schedules your tasks into available time slots. It learns your work patterns, protects deep work blocks, and even reschedules when meetings inevitably change.

**Best for:** Freelancers and knowledge workers who need deep work protection
**Pricing:** $34/m (Starter) / $57/m (Team)
**Platform:** Web, macOS, mobile apps

### Reclaim
Reclaim focuses on defending your calendar habits — lunch breaks, exercise, focus time — while automatically scheduling meetings in the gaps. Its team features let you coordinate availability across colleagues.

**Best for:** Teams that want consistent schedule defense (lunch, exercise, focus)
**Pricing:** Free / $16-30/m (Starter/Plus)
**Platform:** Google Calendar only

### Clockwise
Clockwise optimizes your existing calendar by shifting flexible meetings into "bundles" to create larger blocks of uninterrupted time. It's the least intrusive of the three — it works with your existing calendar rather than replacing it.

**Best for:** Enterprise teams where Calendar is a shared resource
**Pricing:** Free / $15/m (Pro) / Custom (Enterprise)
**Platform:** Google Calendar, Outlook (limited)

## Which One Should You Use?

**Get Motion if:** You're a freelancer, consultant, or knowledge worker who needs to get deep work done. The task-scheduling feature is a game-changer for anyone who struggles to find time for focused work.

**Get Reclaim if:** You work in a team where calendar coordination is a daily pain, or you need automatic defense for personal habits (lunch, gym, pickup). The free tier is generous enough to test.

**Get Clockwise if:** Your organization uses Google Calendar at scale and you want minimal disruption. Clockwise's gentle approach works best in cultures where aggressive calendar automation would cause friction.

## FAQ

**Can these tools automatically decline meetings that conflict with focus time?** Motion and Reclaim can auto-decline. Clockwise moves them to bundling windows instead.

**Do they work across time zones?** All three handle time zone differences well. Motion and Reclaim have the best travel-time awareness.

**Will my calendar look weird to colleagues?** Clockwise's bundling can look unusual (meetings grouped in tight blocks). Motion's task blocks look like busy time. Reclaim's habits show as defended events.

**Is the free tier useful?** Reclaim's free tier is the most generous. Clockwise's free tier gives you Prism (basic focus time). Motion has no free tier but offers a 14-day trial.
