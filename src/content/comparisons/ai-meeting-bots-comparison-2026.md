# AI Meeting Bots Comparison 2026: Otter vs Fireflies vs Fathom vs Krisp

Meetings are simultaneously the most productive and least productive part of modern work. AI meeting bots have emerged as essential tools for capturing, summarizing, and extracting value from the hours we spend in video calls. In 2026, four tools dominate the market: Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai, Fathom, and Krisp.

Each takes a different approach — Otter focuses on real-time transcription and collaboration, Fireflies emphasizes enterprise search across all conversations, Fathom prioritizes meeting summarization and action tracking, and Krisp doubles as both a noise-cancellation tool and a meeting assistant. This comparison evaluates each across the dimensions that matter most: transcription accuracy, action item extraction, platform integration, and value for money.

## Detailed Comparison

### Otter.ai: The Real-Time Collaboration Pioneer

Otter.ai has been in the meeting transcription space longer than any competitor, and it shows in the polish of its real-time transcription and the depth of its collaboration features. It's the only tool that serves as both a meeting bot and a collaboration workspace.

**Pricing & Plans:**
- **Otter Basic (Free):** 300 minutes/month transcription, 30 min/meeting max, basic AI summaries
- **Otter Pro ($16.99/seat/mo, billed annually):** 1,200 minutes/month, 90 min/meeting, advanced AI summaries, custom vocabulary
- **Otter Business ($30/seat/mo, billed annually):** Unlimited minutes, Salesforce integration, priority support, SSO
- **Otter Enterprise ($39/seat/mo):** Custom retention policies, data residency, dedicated support

**Key Capabilities:**
- **Live transcript**: Real-time captions visible during the meeting, with speaker identification
- **Meeting Digest**: Auto-generated summary with bullet points, action items, and key questions
- **AI Chat**: Ask questions about past meetings — "What did Sarah say about the Q3 timeline?"
- **Workspace**: Shared meeting notes with team comments, highlight annotations, and threaded discussions
- **OtterPilot**: Automatic bot joins your calendar meetings; no manual invitations needed
- **Speaker identification**: Learns to recognize recurring participants across meetings
- **Export**: Transcripts and summaries to Notion, Slack, Salesforce, HubSpot, and more

**Pros:**
- Best real-time transcription accuracy in the industry (~95% for clear audio)
- Strong collaboration features — shared workspaces feel like a dedicated notes app
- OtterPilot auto-joins without manual steps
- Long history of reliability and continuous improvement

**Cons:**
- Free tier is limited (300 min/month, 30 min/meeting cap)
- Action item extraction is less sophisticated than Fathom
- Bot joins meetings visibly — can feel intrusive
- Custom vocabulary setup is manual and tedious

**Best Use Case:** Teams that want real-time captions during meetings and a shared collaboration space around meeting notes.

### Fireflies.ai: The Enterprise Conversation Search Engine

Fireflies positions itself as a search engine for your conversations. Rather than just transcribing individual meetings, it indexes everything — meetings, calls, and even transcribed audio recordings — making them searchable across your entire organization.

**Pricing & Plans:**
- **Fireflies Free:** Limited transcription (credits-based), 7-day search history, basic AI summaries
- **Fireflies Pro ($10/seat/mo, billed annually):** Unlimited transcription, unlimited search history, advanced AI summaries
- **Fireflies Business ($19/seat/mo, billed annually):** Custom AI models, Salesforce integration, priority support, analytics dashboard
- **Fireflies Enterprise ($39/seat/mo):** SOC 2 Type II, data residency, dedicated support, custom integrations

**Key Capabilities:**
- **Universal search**: Search across all meetings by keyword, speaker, date, topic — like Google for your conversations
- **Soundbites**: Create shareable 30-second audio clips with animated transcripts
- **Smart Summaries**: Topic-based chapter markers, key takeaways, action items with assignee detection
- **AI Filters**: Filter conversations by sentiment, topic, speaker, meeting type
- **CRM integration**: Two-way sync with Salesforce, HubSpot, PipeDrive — logs meeting notes to contact records
- **Meeting minutes**: Formatted meeting minutes matching your company template
- **Modular add-ons**: Pinpoint (precise quote search), Recap (daily/weekly meeting summaries), Review (collaborative clip review)

**Pros:**
- Best search capability across all meetings
- Soundbites are brilliant for sharing key moments
- Strong CRM integrations for sales teams
- Most affordable Pro tier at $10/seat/mo
- Broadest platform support (Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Webex, RingCentral)

**Cons:**
- No real-time live transcription (only post-meeting processing)
- Interface can feel overwhelming with features
- Search quality degrades with poor audio transcription
- Soundbites can be gimmicky if overused

**Best Use Case:** Sales teams and large enterprises that need to search across thousands of conversations and automatically log meeting insights into CRM systems.

### Fathom: The Meeting Summarization Specialist

Fathom has rapidly gained market share by focusing relentlessly on what happens *after* the meeting — extracting clear actions, decisions, and follow-ups. Its "Smart Links" feature connects meeting outcomes directly to tasks in project management tools.

**Pricing & Plans:**
- **Fathom Free:** Unlimited recording, unlimited summaries, 10+ integrations, unlimited search
- **Fathom Pro ($17/seat/mo, billed annually):** Unlimited AI action items, Smart Links, custom templates, Salesforce integration
- **Fathom Team ($25/seat/mo, billed annually):** Team analytics, admin dashboard, priority support, SSO
- **Fathom Enterprise (Custom):** Custom AI models, dedicated infrastructure, SLA guarantees

**Key Capabilities:**
- **Auto-summarization**: High-quality summaries with timestamps, decisions, and action items
- **Smart Links**: Action items automatically create tasks in Asana, Linear, Jira, Notion, or Monday.com — one click sync
- **Highlight Reel**: Short 60-second video summary of meeting highlights
- **In-meeting view**: Live transcript with ability to highlight and tag in real-time
- **CRM Sync**: Meeting notes and action items sync to Salesforce and HubSpot automatically
- **Fathom AI**: Ask questions about any past meeting — "What was the budget decision?"
- **Meeting templates**: Standardize meeting output format across teams

**Pros:**
- Free tier is genuinely unlimited — no caps on recording or summaries
- Best action item extraction with direct sync to project management tools
- Smart Links is genuinely useful, not just a marketing term
- Clean, focused interface that doesn't overwhelm
- No meeting participant limit on free plan

**Cons:**
- No real-time live captions (post-meeting processing)
- Fewer total integrations than Fireflies
- No native mobile app (web and desktop only)
- Action items can miss context if meeting was unstructured

**Best Use Case:** Teams that want to turn every meeting into tracked, accountable action items with minimal manual effort.

### Krisp: The Privacy-First Meeting Assistant

Krisp takes a fundamentally different approach. It processes audio locally on your device rather than through cloud servers, making it the privacy-friendly choice. It also functions as a real-time noise cancellation tool, which is its original claim to fame.

**Pricing & Plans:**
- **Krisp Free:** 60 minutes/day of transcription, basic noise cancellation
- **Krisp Pro ($8/seat/mo, billed annually):** Unlimited transcription, HD noise cancellation, voice separation
- **Krisp Business ($15/seat/mo, billed annually):** Team management, admin controls, SSO, priority support
- **Krisp Enterprise ($30/seat/mo):** Data residency, custom models, dedicated infrastructure

**Key Capabilities:**
- **On-device AI**: All audio processing happens locally — no cloud upload, no privacy concerns
- **Noise cancellation**: Industry-leading background noise removal (dog barking, construction, cafe noise)
- **Voice separation**: Separates speakers in real-time for cleaner multi-participant audio
- **Post-meeting summaries**: AI-generated summaries with action items (processed locally)
- **Live transcription**: Real-time captions with ~92% accuracy for clear audio
- **Meeting notes**: Auto-generated meeting minutes with key points
- **Krisp Voice (new):** AI voice changer that works in real-time on any meeting platform

**Pros:**
- Privacy-first — no data leaves your device
- Best noise cancellation — transforms chaotic home offices into studio-quality audio
- Cheapest premium tier at $8/seat/mo
- No bot joins the meeting (less intrusive)
- Voice separation is genuinely impressive

**Cons:**
- Lower transcription accuracy (~92% vs 95% for Otter)
- No CRM integrations
- Limited collaboration features
- No cloud search across meetings
- Processed summaries are less detailed than competitors

**Best Use Case:** Privacy-conscious users, remote workers in noisy environments, and anyone who values noise cancellation as much as transcription.

## Head-to-Head by Category

### Transcription Accuracy

In controlled tests with clear audio, **Otter** achieves the highest accuracy at ~95%, thanks to years of training on meeting-specific speech patterns. **Fireflies** is close behind at ~93% for English, with slightly lower accuracy for other languages. **Fathom** achieves ~92% in post-meeting processing. **Krisp's** on-device processing limits its accuracy to ~92% for clear audio and lower in complex acoustic environments.

**Winner:** Otter.ai (95% accuracy)

### Action Item Extraction & Follow-Through

This is the most important metric for many teams. **Fathom** leads with its Smart Links feature — action items aren't just identified, they're automatically created as tasks in your project management tool. **Fireflies** is strong too, with assignee detection and CRM sync. **Otter** identifies potential action items but requires manual confirmation. **Krisp** has basic extraction that sometimes misses context.

**Winner:** Fathom (Smart Links are genuinely transformative)

### Meeting Platform Integration

**Fireflies** supports the widest range of platforms — Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, and RingCentral. **Otter** and **Fathom** support the top three (Zoom, Google Meet, Teams). **Krisp** takes a platform-agnostic approach — it works with any meeting app because it processes audio at the system level rather than joining as a bot — but this means less deep integration with each platform.

**Winner:** Fireflies.ai (broadest platform support)

### Pricing & Value

**Krisp** wins on pure affordability at $8/seat/mo for unlimited transcription. **Fireflies Pro** at $10/seat/mo is remarkable value for what you get — unlimited transcription with strong search. **Fathom's** genuinely unlimited free tier is the best deal for small teams. **Otter** is the most expensive at the Pro level and the free tier is heavily limited.

**Winner:** Fathom (best free tier); Krisp (cheapest paid option)

## Winner by Use Case

- **Best Overall**: **Fathom** — The combination of a genuinely unlimited free tier, best-in-class action item extraction with Smart Links, and clean interface make it the right choice for most teams. It's the only tool that reliably turns meetings into tracked, accountable outcomes.

- **Best Value**: **Fireflies.ai** — At $10/seat/mo for unlimited transcription with excellent search, Fireflies is the most feature-rich option for the price. The CRM integrations make it particularly valuable for sales teams.

- **Best for Privacy**: **Krisp** — If your organization has strict data residency requirements or you're just privacy-conscious, Krisp's on-device processing is the only option that truly keeps your conversations off the cloud.

- **Best for Real-Time**: **Otter.ai** — The only tool with production-quality real-time captions during meetings, plus strong collaboration features around shared meeting notes.

- **Best for Sales Teams**: **Fireflies.ai** — Two-way CRM sync, Soundbites for sharing call moments with prospects, and organization-wide search make it the sales team favorite.

- **Best for Remote Workers**: **Krisp** — The noise cancellation alone is worth the price, and the local transcription is a bonus. Use it alongside any meeting platform without a bot.

But if you need real-time captions, **Otter** is better. If you need organization-wide search across thousands of conversations, **Fireflies** wins. And if privacy or noise cancellation are priorities, **Krisp** is the clear choice. Many teams actually use two — Krisp for noise cancellation and Otter or Fathom for transcription.
