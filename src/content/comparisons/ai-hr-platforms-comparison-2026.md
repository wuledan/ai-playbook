## Quick Overview

HR platforms have evolved far beyond employee databases. Modern AI-powered HR tools handle recruitment, onboarding, payroll, compliance, performance management, and people analytics — all in one place. Rippling, BambooHR, and Workable each take different approaches to this problem.

Rippling is the most comprehensive platform, combining HR with IT device management, app provisioning, and expense management — all unified under one system. BambooHR is the beloved mid-market HRIS with the best employee experience and culture-building features. Workable is purpose-built for recruiting but now extends into core HR with AI-powered hiring workflows. For growing companies that want HR + IT in one platform, Rippling wins. For companies that prioritize employee experience and culture, BambooHR is the gold standard. For high-volume hiring organizations, Workable's AI recruitment engine is unmatched.

### Best for Each Use Case

- **All-in-one HR + IT management:** Rippling — unified platform unmatched
- **Employee experience and culture:** BambooHR — simplest, most loved interface
- **High-volume recruitment:** Workable — best AI sourcing and screening
- **Global / multi-country teams:** Rippling — strongest international capabilities
- **First-time HR system for SMB:** BambooHR — easiest to set up and adopt

## Rippling — HR + IT Unified Platform

Rippling connects HR with IT, finance, and operations in a single platform. When you hire someone, Rippling automatically creates their payroll entry, provisions their laptop (via integration with Apple Business Manager or Dell), sets up their apps (Gmail, Slack, Zoom), and assigns training. When you terminate, it reverses all of this instantly. The AI layer helps with compliance monitoring, compensation benchmarking, and employee sentiment analysis.

**Strengths:**
- Unmatched HR + IT integration
- Automated onboarding/offboarding across apps
- Global payroll in 185+ countries
- Strong compliance automation
- Unified data model across all modules

**Weaknesses:**
- Can be overwhelming for companies under 50 employees
- Higher price point per employee
- Implementation takes 4-8 weeks
- Some features feel like separate products stitched together

**Best for:** Mid-to-large companies that want HR, IT, and finance tightly integrated.

## BambooHR — The Employee Experience Leader

BambooHR focuses on making HR software that people actually enjoy using. The interface is clean, intuitive, and designed for employees rather than HR admins. Features include automated onboarding checklists, time-off requests that actually work, performance review cycles, and an employee directory that serves as the company org chart. The AI features include candidate matching, sentiment analysis from eNPS surveys, and automated compliance alerts.

**Strengths:**
- Best user experience in HR software
- Excellent employee self-service portal
- Clean, modern interface
- Great for culture-building features
- Quick implementation (2-4 weeks)

**Weaknesses:**
- No IT device management or app provisioning
- Payroll requires add-on (BambooHR Payroll, US only)
- Limited customization for complex workflows
- Weaker for global teams (US-focused)

**Best for:** SMBs and mid-market companies that prioritize employee experience and simple, effective HR.

## Workable — AI-Powered Hiring Platform

Workable started as an applicant tracking system and has evolved into a full HR platform. Its AI capabilities are strongest in the recruiting domain — AI sourcing finds candidates across 200+ job boards automatically, AI screening evaluates resumes against job requirements, and AI matching ranks candidates by fit score. The platform also handles onboarding, time-off tracking, performance management, and people analytics.

**Strengths:**
- Best AI candidate sourcing and screening
- Largest job board distribution network
- Excellent assessment and evaluation tools
- Strong collaboration features for hiring teams
- Good candidate experience and communication

**Weaknesses:**
- No payroll capabilities
- Core HR features feel secondary to recruiting
- Limited customization for non-recruiting workflows
- Higher cost per hire for small teams

**Best for:** Companies that hire frequently (10+ hires/year) and want the best AI recruitment tools.

## When to Use Each

- **Use Rippling** if you want HR, IT, and finance under one roof with global capabilities
- **Use BambooHR** if employee experience and culture are your top HR priorities
- **Use Workable** if recruitment volume is your primary challenge and you need the best AI hiring tools
- **Use Rippling + Workable** if you need both strong recruitment and full HR/IT management
- **Use BambooHR + a separate ATS** if you like BambooHR for HR but need stronger recruiting

## FAQ

**Which is best for a 50-person company?** BambooHR offers the best experience-to-cost ratio. Rippling may be overkill at this size. Workable is great if you're actively growing.

**Can Rippling really manage IT and HR together?** Yes — it's the most integrated platform on the market. When you hire an engineer, Rippling can automatically order a laptop, create their GitHub and Slack accounts, and enroll them in benefits.

**Does BambooHR work for global teams?** BambooHR is primarily US-focused. For global teams (multi-country employees, multiple currencies), Rippling is the better choice.

**Which has the best AI recruiting features?** Workable leads with AI sourcing, screening, and candidate matching. Rippling's AI recruiting is improving but isn't as mature as Workable's.

**What about customer support?** BambooHR has the highest customer satisfaction scores. Rippling's support is good but slower for complex issues. Workable's support is responsive with dedicated account management for higher tiers.
