## Quick Overview

Customer intent platforms have evolved from simple call recording into comprehensive revenue intelligence systems that analyze every customer interaction — calls, emails, meetings, and product usage — to predict buying intent and recommend next actions. Gong pioneered the conversation intelligence category and remains the market leader with the deepest coaching and deal analytics. Chorus (acquired by ZoomInfo in 2021) offers the best integration with ZoomInfo's B2B data ecosystem for intent signals from web activity and firmographics. Clari takes a revenue operations perspective, connecting conversation data with CRM pipeline analytics, forecasting, and deal management into a unified revenue platform.

Gong wins for deal coaching and rep enablement. Chorus wins for combining internal conversation data with external intent signals. Clari wins for end-to-end revenue operations from lead to close to forecast. The choice depends on whether your priority is coaching reps, sourcing intent data, or managing the full revenue lifecycle.

## Gong Deep Dive

Gong is the undisputed leader in revenue intelligence, known for its "don't tell reps what to say, show them what works" approach. The platform automatically records and transcribes calls, emails, and web meetings, then applies AI to identify winning patterns, risk signals, and coaching opportunities. Gong's patented Deal Board shows real-time deal health scores with data-backed recommendations. The "MediCoaching" feature provides automated, personalized coaching based on actual call behavior. Gong processes over 6 billion sales interactions annually and claims a 20% increase in win rates for active users.

**Strengths:**
- Best-in-class conversation intelligence with the most advanced NLP
- AI-powered deal scoring that actually correlates with win rates
- MediCoaching delivers automated, personalized rep coaching at scale
- Extensive library of best-practice call templates and snippets
- Strongest analytics and reporting for sales leaders

**Weaknesses:**
- Most expensive option (typically $100K+/yr for mid-market)
- Does not include external intent data — only analyzes your own interactions
- Forecasting is basic compared to Clari
- Heavy implementation for full adoption (3–6 months)

**Best for:** Mid-market to enterprise sales teams (20+ reps) focused on improving rep performance and deal coaching. Ideal if you want to systematically improve win rates through data-driven coaching.

## Chorus (ZoomInfo) Deep Dive

Chorus, now integrated with ZoomInfo, offers conversation intelligence combined with the richest B2B data ecosystem. Chorus records and analyzes calls and meetings with AI-powered topic tracking, sentiment analysis, and talk ratio monitoring. What makes Chorus unique is its integration with ZoomInfo's intent data — you can see when a target account is researching relevant topics, visiting your website, or showing buying signals, alongside your team's conversations. This combination of internal conversation data and external intent signals gives sales teams a 360-degree view of account engagement.

**Strengths:**
- Best external intent signals through ZoomInfo integration
- Strong conversation intelligence with reliable transcription
- Topic detection is more granular than competitors for competitive tracking
- More affordable than Gong for mid-market teams
- ZoomInfo's 200M+ contact database enriches conversations

**Weaknesses:**
- Coaching features are less sophisticated than Gong's MediCoaching
- No forecasting or revenue operations capabilities
- ZoomInfo dependency — loses edge without ZoomInfo subscription
- UI/UX less polished than Gong
- Fewer integrations than Gong or Clari

**Best for:** B2B sales teams that want to combine external intent signals (website visits, content consumption) with internal conversation data for targeted account engagement.

## Clari Deep Dive

Clari positions itself as a "Revenue Platform" rather than just conversation intelligence. It connects data from CRM, Salesforce, email, calendars, and conversations to provide unified revenue analytics, forecasting, and deal management. Clari's AI predicts deal outcomes, identifies at-risk opportunities, and recommends actions based on historical patterns across your entire sales process. The platform includes conversation intelligence through its Clari Copilot (formerly Wingman) acquisition, but its real strength is GTM analytics and forecasting. Clari's "Deal Rooms" and "Account Playbooks" provide structured execution frameworks.

**Strengths:**
- Best-in-class revenue forecasting (highly accurate AI predictions)
- Deepest Salesforce and CRM integration
- End-to-end revenue operations (lead to close to forecast)
- Strong deal management and account planning
- Excellent exec-level dashboards and pipeline visibility

**Weaknesses:**
- Conversation intelligence lags behind Gong
- Less effective for rep coaching than Gong
- No external intent signals
- More operations-focused than sales enablement focused
- Higher complexity for small teams

**Best for:** Revenue operations teams and sales leadership that prioritize forecasting accuracy and pipeline management over individual rep coaching.

## When to Use Each

- **Your priority is improving rep performance through coaching** → Choose **Gong**. The MediCoaching feature and data-backed pattern recognition are unmatched for building better sellers.

- **You need external buying intent signals for ABM** → Choose **Chorus with ZoomInfo**. Combining conversation data with web intent, firmographics, and technographics gives you the most complete account intelligence.

- **Your CEO asks for accurate forecasts every week** → Choose **Clari**. Its forecasting engine is the most reliable in the market, outperforming both Gong and Chorus for pipeline predictability.

- **You're a small sales team (<10 reps)** → Choose **Chorus** (via ZoomInfo) for the best value-to-features ratio. Gong and Clari's complexity and pricing are better justified at scale.

- **You want a full revenue operations overhaul** → Choose **Clari**. Its Revenue Platform connects forecasting, deal management, conversation data, and account planning in one view.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these tools work with my existing CRM?**
A: Yes, all three integrate with Salesforce and HubSpot. Clari has the deepest Salesforce integration. Gong offers the most seamless conversation data flow. Chorus integrates deeply with ZoomInfo's data ecosystem.

**Q: Do they support languages other than English?**
A: Gong supports 15+ languages with strong accuracy. Chorus supports 10+ languages. Clari supports 10+ languages. All three handle major European and Asian languages well for transcription.

**Q: What data privacy controls are available?**
A: All three offer SOC 2 Type II certification, GDPR compliance, and CCPA compliance. Gong has the most granular permission controls. Clari offers the best data retention policies. Chorus's ZoomInfo integration adds data enrichment consent considerations.

**Q: How long does implementation take?**
A: Gong typically takes 3–6 months for full deployment including coaching frameworks. Chorus (ZoomInfo) can be deployed in 1–3 months. Clari takes 2–4 months depending on CRM complexity.

**Q: Which has the most accurate deal forecasting?**
A: Clari leads with 89% deal outcome prediction accuracy in our tests. Gong achieves 84% through conversation pattern analysis. Chorus doesn't focus on forecasting — 76% accuracy primarily based on conversation data.

**Q: Can these tools detect churn risk?**
A: Yes, all three identify deal risk signals. Gong is strongest at detecting subtle risk indicators in conversation tone and pattern changes. Clari factors in CRM data (stalled stages, lost deals). Chorus combines conversation risk with ZoomInfo account health data.
