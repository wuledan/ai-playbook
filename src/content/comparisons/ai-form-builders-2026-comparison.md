### Real Test: "Build a customer satisfaction survey"

We gave each tool the same prompt: "Create a customer satisfaction survey with NPS, service rating, open feedback, and conditional follow-up for detractors."

- **Jotform AI:** Generated a 12-question form in 8 seconds with full logic. Added branding automatically. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Typeform AI:** Generated a beautiful 10-question form in 15 seconds. Design was best, but logic setup was simpler. ⭐⭐⭐⭐
- **Tally:** Generated a basic 8-question form. Had to add logic manually. Clean interface though. ⭐⭐⭐

## FAQ

**Can AI forms handle complex logic?** Jotform's AI does best with branching logic. Typeform handles basic logic well. Tally requires manual setup for anything beyond simple conditions.

**Which is best for lead generation?** Jotform's CRM integrations make it the best choice for capturing and routing leads. Typeform is better when form design impacts conversion rates.

**Is the free tier useful?** Tally's free tier is the most generous (unlimited forms, responses). Jotform offers 100 submissions/mo free. Typeform limits free forms to 10 questions and 100 responses.
