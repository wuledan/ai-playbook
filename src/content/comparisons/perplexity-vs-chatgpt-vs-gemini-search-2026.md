5. **Complex analysis** (4) — multi-source synthesis, opinion-based

## FAQ

**Q: Which has the most up-to-date information?**
A: Perplexity Pro consistently has the most recent and well-sourced information. Gemini Search struggles with real-time updates. ChatGPT Search is somewhere in between.

**Q: Can I trust these for academic research?**
A: Perplexity Pro is the most trustworthy due to its source citation system. Always verify original sources regardless of which tool you use.

**Q: Is Gemini Search really free?**
A: Yes, Gemini Search is free with a Google account. The premium Gemini Advanced adds 1M context and priority access but the search function is identical.
